_[[Cantrip]]_

**Casting**:: [[Double Action]]
**Components**:: V, S
**Mana**:: 0

**Range**:: 40 metres
**Duration**:: Instant

**Effect**:: You try insert mind psychic spike to mind of targe. Choose one target you can see within range. Each target makes [[Charisma Saving Throw]]:

**Success:** Target takes no damage.
**Failure:** Target takes 1d8 [[Psychic Damage]].
**Critical Failure:** Target takes 1d8 [[Psychic Damage]] and can't have [[Reaction|Reactions]] until the end of your next turn.

**Upcasting**:: For each additional mana spent, the damage increases by 1d6. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 3 extra mana.
