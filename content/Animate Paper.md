_[[Cantrip]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: S, M\*
**Mana**:: 0
**Range**:: Touch
**Duration**:: [[Concentration]], 10 minutes

_\*[[Parchment Sheet]]._

**Effect**:: You imbue sheet of parchment with life. Parchment animates into a small, origami like [[Creature]] with simple movements. See [[Animated Paper]] for full statistics.

While animated, a piece of parchment can:

- Fly up to 5 metres per turn.
- Follow simple verbal commands from you.

**Upcasting**:: For each 1 additional mana spent, you can affect additional sheet of parchment.

**Type**:: [[Summon Magic]]
