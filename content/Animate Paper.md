_[[Cantrip]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: S, M\*
**Mana**:: 0

**Range**:: Touch
**Duration**:: 10 minutes

_\*Sheet of Parchment_

**Effect**:: You imbue sheets of parchment with temporary life. Parchment animates into a small, origami creature with simple movements. See [[Animated Paper]] for full statistics.

While animated, a piece of parchment can:

- Fly up to 20 metres per turn.
- Follow simple verbal commands from you.

**Upcasting**:: For each additional mana spent, you can affect additional sheet of parchment. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 3 extra mana.
