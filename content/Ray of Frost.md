_[[Cantrip]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: F, V, S
**Mana**:: 0

**Range**:: 40 metres
**Duration**:: Instant

**Effect**:: You form ice beam and aim it towards your enemy. Choose one target you can see within range. Target makes [[Agility Saving Throw]]:

**Success:** Target takes no damage.
**Failure:** Target takes 1d10 [[Cold Damage]].

**Upcasting**:: For each additional mana spent, the damage increases by 1d6. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 3 extra mana.

**Type**:: [[Evocation Magic]]
