_[[Alchemical Item]]_

_Very fine, icy, star-shaped crystals._

**Cost**:: 1 gp
**Weight**:: 0.1 kg
**Duration**:: Instant

As a [[Double Action]], you can sprinkle one dose of [[Freeze Powder]] onto a liquid, instantly freezing up to 1,000 litres of it. Other substances become covered in frost and extremely cold.

Alternatively, you can sprinkle the powder at one [[Creature]] within 1 metre of you. The target must make a [[Constitution Saving Throw]].

**Critical Success:** No effect.
**Success:** The target takes half damage.
**Failure:** The target takes 2d12 [[Cold Damage]].
**Critical Failure:** The target takes double damage, and its [[Walking Speed]] is halved until the start of your next [[Turn]].
