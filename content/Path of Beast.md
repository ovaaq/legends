**Requirements**:: [[Spiritual Connection]]
**Cost**:: 1 LP

_Flavour text_.

You can summon a nature spirit that assumes an animal form to aid you. As an [[Double Action]], you can spend 1 Spirit Point to cast the [[Find Familiar]] spell without [[Spell Parts]].

When you cast the spell in this way, the familiar is Fey and disappears when you finish a [[Long Rest]].
