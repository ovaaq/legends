When you wield this weapon with two hands, its [[Attack]] costs 1 additional [[Action]], and its damage becomes the value indicated by [[Versatile]].

For example, a weapon with an [[Attack]] cost of [[Double Action]] and Versatile (2d10 + [[Strength]]) has an [[Attack]] cost of [[Triple Action]] when wielded with two hands, instead of [[Double Action]], and deals 2d10 + [[Strength]] damage.
