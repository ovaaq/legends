
[[Simple Weapon Training]] -> you can wield simple weapons
[[Advanced Weapon Training]] -> you can wield advanced weapons
[[Simple Focus Training]] -> you can wield 1h focus
[[Advanced Focus Training]] -> you can wield 2h focus ( eg. staff) or 1h focus + 1h focus (eg. wand + wand)
[[Shield Training]] -> you can wield shields

[[Somatic Spell Part]] can be done with hand wielding focus or empty hand
[[Advanced Focus]] provides +1 bonus to spellcasting modifier

dual wielding
- 2x light weapons ok
- 1h weapon + shield ok
- 1h weapon + 1h focus ok
[[Dual Wielder]] -> you can dual wield one light & one non-light weapon (eg. dagger + warhammer)
[[Twinblade Master]] -> you can wield two non-light weapons (eg. warhammer + warhammer)
[[Arbalist]] -> you can wield two 1h ranged weapons (eg. 2x hand crossbows)
