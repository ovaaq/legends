## Select Equipment

[[Equipment]] is an essential part of any adventurer, providing the tools and items needed for exploration, combat, and survival. When starting an adventure, you purchase equipment using the funds granted by your chosen [[Background]]. Any remaining money can be saved as [[Money|Coins]] or [[Gemstone|Gemstones]].

For a quicker start, you may also choose from the available [[Premade Packs]].

### Buying Starting Equipment

You can select any item group to view its contents and purchase items at their listed prices.

|Item Group|Description|
|---|---|
|[[Alchemical Item\|Alchemical Items]]|Potions, elixirs, and other substances with magical or practical effects.|
|[[Adventuring Item\|Adventuring Items]]|General gear used for exploration, survival, and travel.|
|[[Armour]]|Protective gear that reduces damage and improves survivability in combat.|
|[[Tool\|Tools]]|Kits and implements for crafting, repairing, or performing specialized tasks (e.g., lockpicking, artisan work).|
|[[Weapon\|Weapons]]|Melee and ranged arms used in combat or hunting.|

### Premade Packs

Premade [[Pack|Packs]] are designed for convenience, offering ready-made sets of equipment for characters who prefer not to manage every item individually. You can also purchase a Premade Pack and customize it by adding or swapping items as needed.
