Wizards 
- unlock spells by adding them to your [[Wizardry Spell List]]
- learn spells from your [[Spell List]] to your [[Spellbook]]
- each day selects spells to your [[Prepared Spell]]
- you can also ritual cast from your [[Spellbook]]

Pact / Inborn
- unlock spells by adding them to your [[Pact Spell List]] / [[Inborn Spell List]]
- learn spells from your [[Spell List]] to your [[Known Spell]]
- cast spells from your [[Known Spell]]

Divine
- unlock spells by adding them to your [[Divine Spell List]]
- each day select x spells from your [[Spell List]] to be your [[Prepared Spell]]
- cast [[Prepared Spell]]

Spiritual
- unlock spells by adding them to your [[Spiritual Spell List]]

\\\\
Arcane spellcasters are typically scholars who study pure arcane forces using logic and symbolism, and often represent the wizard and artificer archetypes.
\\\\
Divine spellcasters, on the other hand, form a connection to divine deities in order to manipulate the forces of the world, and are often associated with the cleric, priest, and paladin archetypes.
\\\\
Inborn spellcasters possess an innate connection to the arcane, often through a combination of heritage and mysterious circumstances, making their spellcasting more intuitive. This type of spellcasting is often associated with the sorcerer and spellblood archetypes.
\\\\
Pact spellcasting involves making deals with higher beings to borrow magical powers, allowing the spellcaster to control the magic as it happens. This type of spellcasting is associated with the warlock archetype.
\\\\
Spiritual spellcasting involves communicating with spirits to harness their magical powers, often in the form of natural forces, and is associated with druid and shaman archetypes.
\\\\
