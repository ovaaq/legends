### Making Checks

[[Check]]

- [[General Skill]]
- [[Expert Skill]]

**Check** = d20 + [[Ability Score]] + [[General Skill]] [[Rank]] + possible [[Expert Skill]] [[Rank]]

For example:

- Scan Surroundings = d20 + 3([[Awareness]]) + 2([[Perception]] [[Rank]])
- Pickpocket Someone = d20 + 3([[Precision]]) + 2([[Sleight of Hand]] [[Rank]])
- Recall History of a weapon = d20 + 2([[Intelligence]]) + 4([[History]] [[Rank]]) + 2([[Smithing]] [[Rank]])

### Making Saving Throws

[[Saving Throw]]

**Saving Throw** = d20 + [[Ability Score]] + [[Saving Throw]] [[Rank]]

For example:

- Agility Saving Throw = d20 + 3([[Agility]]) + 2([[Agility Saving Throw]] [[Rank]])

#### Making Attack Roll

[[Attack Roll]]

**Weapon Attack Roll** = d20 + [[Strength]] / [[Precision]] + [[Martial Skill]] [[Rank]]
**Spell Attack Roll** = d20 + [[Sorcery]] + [[Spellcasting Skill]] [[Rank]]

For example:

- Hitting with Mace = d20 + 3([[Strength]]) + 2([[Martial Skill]] [[Rank]])
- Stabbing with a Dagger = d20 + 2([[Precision]]) + 4([[Martial Skill]] [[Rank]])
- Casting Fire Bolt = d20 + 3([[Sorcery]]) + 2([[Spellcasting Skill]] [[Rank]])

#### Critical Hit

You score a [[Critical Hit]]:

- When you [[Attack Roll]] is 10 higher than the [[Armour Class]] / [[Evasion Class]].
- You roll [[Natural 20]] and hit the target.

#### Damage Roll

[[Damage Roll]]

- [[Damage Type]]

**Weapon Damage** = Weapon's Damage Die + [[Strength]] / [[Precision]] / Weapon's Damage Type
**Spell Damage** = Spell's Damage Die / Spell's Damage Type

For example:

- Damage with Mace = 1d8 + 3([[Strength]]) [[Bludgeoning Damage]]
- Stabbing with a Dagger = 1d4 + 2([[Precision]]) [[Piercing Damage]]
- Casting Fire Bolt = 3d4 [[Fire Damage]]

#### Damage Roll with Critical Hit

Normal [[Damage Roll]] + maximum from the Damage Dice.

For example:

- Critical Hit with Mace = 1d8 + 3([[Strength]]) + 8(1d8 Maxed) [[Bludgeoning Damage]]
- Critical Hit with a Dagger = 1d4 + 2([[Precision]]) + 4(1d4 Maxed) [[Piercing Damage]]
- Critical Hit Fire Bolt = 3d4 + 12(3d4 Maxed) [[Fire Damage]]

### Resting

[[Resting]]

- [[Short Rest]]
- [[Long Rest]]

#### Short Rest

During a [[Short Rest]], you regain:

- [[Hit Point|Hit Points]] equal to your [[Constitution]].
- [[Stamina Point|Stamina Points]] equal to your [[Constitution]].
- [[Mana Point]] equal to your [[Sorcery]].
- Any additional resources specified by your Talents.

#### Long Rest

- The first 7 hours of a [[Long Rest]] grant each [[Short Rest]] benefits.
- The 8th hour grants additional Long Rest benefits.
  - Regain half of your [[Hit Die|Hit Dice]]
  - Your Talents determine which resources are fully restored.
- You must sleep for at least 6 of the 8 hours to gain Long Rest benefits.
