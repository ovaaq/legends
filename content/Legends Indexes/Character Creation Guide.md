_Legends_ is a classless [[TTRPG]] that gives you the freedom to create unique characters. Instead of a traditional class system, _Legends_ uses [[Talent|Talents]] organized into [[Talent Tree|Talent Trees]], which you can combine in any way you like. Your challenge is to combine [[Talent|Talents]] in creative and exciting ways, producing concepts such as a _potion-brewing shaman warrior_ or a _god-devoted demon hunter_.

When creating a 1. Level character, you have a total of 20 [[Legend Point|Legend Points]]. Five must be allocated to your [[Ancestry Talent|Ancestry Talents]], while the remaining 15 can be spent on any [[Talent]] for which you meet the requirements.

Your character is defined by more than just [[Talent|Talents]]. They also have [[Ability Score|Ability Scores]], an [[Ancestry]], a [[Background]], [[Skill|Skills]], and [[Equipment]], all of which are recorded on your [[Character Sheet]]. Each of these elements will be explained in detail later in this guide. You also have full control over your character’s appearance, name, personality, and personal history, allowing them to fit seamlessly into the story. After reading this guide, you can see [[Filling Character Sheet]] page for a summary of character sheet statistics.

> [!info] Legend Formula
> Character = [[Ability Score|Ability Scores]] + [[Ancestry]] + [[Background]] + [[Skill|Skills]] + [[Saving Throw|Saving Throws]] + [[Talent|Talents]] + [[Equipment]]

## Character Creation 101

You may create your character in any order, but the following sequence is recommended for clarity and convenience.

> [!NOTE] Character Creation at a Glance
>
> 1. Determine Ability Scores
> 2. Choose an Ancestry
> 3. Choose a Background
> 4. Improve Skills & Saving Throws
> 5. Spend 15 Legend Points on Talents
> 6. Purchase Equipment

### 1. Determine Ability Scores

[[Ability Score|Ability Scores]] represent your character’s natural talents and capabilities. There are eight Ability Scores, such as [[Strength]] and [[Intelligence]], that measure how well your character performs different kinds of tasks.

You generate these scores using the [[Standard Array]], rolling dice, or the [[Point Buy]] method, and record them on your [[Character Sheet]]. Your chosen Ancestry may modify one or more Ability Scores. As your character advances, you can further improve these scores by gaining [[Talent|Talents]].

### 2. Choose Ancestry

Your character’s [[Ancestry]] defines their general physical traits and ancestral heritage. You must select one [[Ancestry|Common Ancestry]] from [[Human Ancestry]], [[Dwarven Ancestry]], [[Elven Ancestry]], or [[Halfling Ancestry]]. This choice influences your character’s lifespan, appearance, [[Creature Size]] and [[Speed]]. Ancestry also unlocks [[Ancestry Talent|Ancestral Talents]].

You may also choose an additional ancestry to represent mixed heritage. This choice is entirely optional. An additional ancestry can be selected from the [[Ancestry|Common Ancestries]] listed above or from the [[Ancestry|Rare Ancestries]], which currently include [[Giant Ancestry]], [[Celestial Ancestry]], and [[Infernal Ancestry]]. This option finally allows _half-dwarf_ characters.

After choosing your [[Ancestry]], you spend 5 [[Legend Point|Legend Points]] to acquire [[Ancestry Talent|Ancestral Talents]].

### 3. Select Background

Choosing a [[Background]] answers the question: _What did your character do before beginning their adventuring career?_ Your character may have been a [[Blacksmith]] or a [[Merchant]]. This choice improves few [[General Skill|General Skills]] and [[Expert Skill|Expert Skills]], grants one or two [[Talent|Talents]], improves your [[Ability Score|Ability Scores]], grants to your [[Rank|Ranks]] and gives starting [[Equipment]] and [[Money]].

### 4. Improve Skills & Saving Throw

[[Skill|Skills]] represent a character’s trained abilities. They are divided into two categories: [[General Skill|General Skills]] and [[Expert Skill|Expert Skills]].

General Skills cover common activities that most adventurers can attempt, such as [[Investigation]], [[History]], or [[Acrobatics]]. Expert Skills represent highly specialized disciplines, such as [[Lockpicking]] or [[Musicality]], that are extremely difficult or impossible to use effectively without proper training.

Each Skill has a [[Rank]], which measures your character’s level of proficiency. Higher Ranks allow characters to tackle more complex challenges and improve their likelihood of success. Each Rank grants a +2 bonus to the associated [[Check]].

[[Saving Throw|Saving Throws]] function in a similar way to General Skills. Each Saving Throw has its own [[Rank]], which represents a character’s resilience against specific effects. Every Rank provides a +2 bonus to the relevant [[Saving Throw]].

### 5. Select Talents

Choosing [[Talent|Talents]] is the most important part of character creation. Talents define what your character can do, whether that is [[Advanced Weapon Training|swing a sword]], [[Divine Acolyte|cast a divine spell]], or [[Alchemy|brew a potion]]. You begin the game with 15 [[Legend Point|Legend Points]], which are used to acquire new Talents. You gain additional Legend Points each time your character levels up.

Some Talents have prerequisites, such as a specific [[Ancestry]], [[Character Level]], known [[Language]], or other Talent. Aside from these requirements, you are free to build your character however you choose. Talents are organized into [[Talent Tree|Talent Trees]], making it easier to plan how future Legend Points can be spent.

### 6. Select Equipment

Using the [[Money]] granted by your chosen [[Background]], you can purchase [[Adventuring Item|Adventuring Items]], [[Armour]], or [[Weapon|Weapons]]. Any remaining funds may be saved as [[Money]] or [[Gemstone|Gemstones]]. If you want to start quickly, you may also choose from the available [[Premade Packs]].

## Determine Ability Scores

When creating a character, you assign values to eight [[Ability Score|Ability Scores]], four physical and four mental. These abilities are: [[Strength]], [[Precision]], [[Agility]], [[Constitution]], [[Awareness]], [[Charisma]], [[Intelligence]] and [[Sorcery]]. Each Ability Score ranges from -5 to +5, where 0 represents the average level in the human population. Since this is a game of legends, your character’s Ability Scores are expected to exceed this average, enabling extraordinary feats and capabilities.

|  Ability Score   | **Abbr.** |   Type   |                                                                            Description                                                                             |
| :--------------: | :-------: | :------: | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------: |
|   [[Strength]]   |    Str    | Physical |           Represents raw muscular power. Key for characters relying on brute strength, such as berserk warriors, armored knights, or athletic wrestlers.           |
|  [[Precision]]   |    Pre    | Physical |      Reflects fine motor control and accuracy. Vital for characters who rely on precise strikes, such as quick-handed burglars, skilled archers, or crafters.      |
|   [[Agility]]    |    Agi    | Physical |        Measures reflexes and coordination. Important for characters who rely on mobility and dodging, such as acrobats, sneaky outlaws, or nimble fighters.        |
| [[Constitution]] |    Con    | Physical |                                Represents stamina and resilience. Crucial for adventurers enduring harsh conditions or sicknesses.                                 |
|  [[Awareness]]   |    Awa    |  Mental  | Measures awareness, intuition, and perceptiveness. Important for characters who rely on keen senses, such as scouts, sharp-eyed archers, or cautious conspirators. |
|   [[Charisma]]   |    Cha    |  Mental  |    Reflects mental toughness and force of personality. Essential for persuasive, cunning, or mentally resilient adventurers, like diplomats, bards, or slayers.    |
| [[Intelligence]] |    Int    |  Mental  |        Represents reasoning, memory, and knowledge. Key for characters who rely on intellect, like scholars, spellcasters, investigators, or lore masters.         |
|   [[Sorcery]]    |    Sor    |  Mental  |                                   Reflects connection to magical forces and the ability to harness them. Vital for spellcasters.                                   |

### How Ability Scores Affect Your Character

#### Strength (Str)

- **Skills:** Add Strength to [[Athletics]] checks
- **Saving Throws:** Add Strength to [[Strength Saving Throw]]
- **Equipment Use:** Determines your ability to wield heavy weapons and wear heavy armour
- **Melee Combat:** Add Strength to [[Melee Weapon Attack]] and [[Damage Roll]]
- **Martial Techniques:** Your [[Martial Save DC]] equals 8 + [[Martial Skill]] + Strength
- **Carrying Capacity:** Your total [[Carrying Capacity]] equals  10 + 2 × Strength

#### Precision (Pre)

- **Skills:** Add Precision to [[Crafting]] and [[Sleight of Hand]] checks
- **Melee Combat:** Add Precision to [[Melee Weapon Attack]] and [[Damage Roll]] if you are using [[Finesse]] [[Weapon]]
- **Ranged Combat:** Add Precision to [[Ranged Weapon Attack]] and [[Damage Roll]]
- **Combat:** [[Martial Save DC]] = 8 + [[Martial Skill]] + Precision

#### Agility (Agi)

- **Skills:** Add Agility to [[Acrobatics]] and [[Stealth]] checks
- **Saving Throw:** Add Agility to [[Agility Saving Throw]]
- **Dodge:** [[Evasion Class]] = 10 + [[Agility]]

#### Constitution (Con)

- **Saving Throw:** Add Constitution to [[Constitution Saving Throw]]
- **Hit Points:** Starting [[Hit Point Maximum]] = 12 + Constitution
- **Recovery:** Constitution determines [[Hit Die]]
- **Endurance:** Constitution determines how long you can [[Hold Breath]] or [[Dash]]

#### Awareness (Awa)

- **Skills:** Add Awareness to [[Animal Handling]], [[Insight]], [[Perception]], [[Spirituality]], [[Streetwise]] and [[Survival]] checks
- **Keen Eye:** [[Passive Perception]] equals 10 + [[Perception]]
- **Observant:** [[Passive Insight]] equals 10 + [[Insight]]

#### Charisma (Cha)

- **Skills:** Add Charisma to [[Deception]], [[Intimidation]], [[Performance]] and [[Persuasion]] checks
- **Saving Throw:** Add Charisma to [[Charisma Saving Throw]]

#### Intelligence (Int)

- **Skills:** Add Intelligence to [[Arcana]], [[History]], [[Investigation]], [[Medicine]], [[Nature]] and [[Religion]] checks
- **Saving Throw:** Add Intelligence to [[Intelligence Saving Throw]]
- **Prepared Spells:** Increases the number of [[Prepared Spell|Prepared Spells]]
- **Languages:** An Intelligence of -3 or lower prevents reading and writing
- **Deduction:** [[Passive Investigation]] = 10 + [[Investigation]]

#### Sorcery (Sor)

- **Combat:** Add Sorcery to [[Spell Attack|Spell Attacks]]
- **Combat:** [[Spell Save DC]] equals 8 + [[Spellcasting Skill]] + [[Sorcery]]
- **Mana:** Starting [[Mana Maximum]] equals [[Sorcery]]

### Using Standard Array

The standard array in _Legends_ is **+3, +2, +2, +1, +1, +0, -1, -2**. You can assign these values to any of your character’s eight [[Ability Score|Ability Scores]], distributing them however you like. Once assigned, record the finalized Ability Scores on your [[Character Sheet]]. This array is designed to provide a mix of strengths and weaknesses, encouraging balanced and varied gameplay.

### Dice Rolls & Point Buy

Check with your local [[Game Master]] for guidance on dice rolls and [[Point Buy]] rules.

## Select Ancestry

Your character’s [[Ancestry]] defines general physical traits and ancestral heritage. You must select one [[Ancestry|Common Ancestry]] from [[Human Ancestry]], [[Dwarven Ancestry]], [[Elven Ancestry]], or [[Halfling Ancestry]]. This choice influences your character’s lifespan, appearance, [[Creature Size]] and [[Speed]]. Ancestry also unlocks [[Ancestry Talent|Ancestral Talents]].

You may optionally choose an additional ancestry to represent mixed heritage. This choice can be made from the [[Ancestry|Common Ancestries]] listed above or from the [[Ancestry|Rare Ancestries]], which currently include [[Giant Ancestry]], [[Celestial Ancestry]], and [[Infernal Ancestry]].

After selecting your [[Ancestry|Common Ancestry]] and any additional ancestry, you spend 5 Legend Points to [[Ancestry|Ancestry Talents]] based on the options your ancestry provides. Below, we detail the Common Ancestries and how they begin shaping your character.

> [!info] **[[Dwarven Ancestry]]**
> _Carved from the heart of stone itself, dwarves are a long-lived and enduring folk. Their bond with the earth runs deep; stone, metal, and gems feel as much a part of them as flesh and blood. Though their origins lie in the deep places of the world, dwarves have spread far across the realms, adapting to mountain strongholds, bustling cities, and distant frontiers alike._
>
> **Long-lived.** Dwarves mature at roughly the same pace as humans but are not considered adults until the age of 50. On average, they live about 350 years.

**Stout and Sturdy.** Dwarves stand between 1.2 and 1.5 meters tall, weighing around 70 kilograms on average. Their compact builds are dense with muscle and bone, granting them remarkable endurance. Your [[Creature Size]] is Medium.

>

**Slow Stride.** Your Walking Speed is 6 meters.

> [!info] **[[Elven Ancestry]]**
> _Elves are a graceful and long-lived people, closely attuned to the rhythms of nature and the flow of magic. Their lives are intertwined with forests, glades, and hidden groves, and though their origins are ancient and mysterious, elves now dwell wherever the natural world thrives, blending seamlessly with its beauty and secrets._

**Long-Lived.** Elves mature more slowly than humans and are not considered adults until around the age of 100. On average, they live about 700 years.

>

**Tall and Slender.** Elves stand between 1.5 and 1.9 meters tall, weighing around 50 kilograms on average. Your [[Creature Size]] is Medium.

>

**Standard Stride.** Your [[Walking Speed]] is 7 meters.

> [!info] **[[Halfling Ancestry]]**
> _Smallfolk are nimble and cheerful folk, drawn to gentle hills, fertile farmland, and the comforts of home. Their small size and light build make them quick and agile, able to slip unnoticed through crowded streets or dense undergrowth. Though often underestimated, their cleverness, resilience, and love of community allow them to thrive in nearly any corner of the world, spreading joy and resourcefulness wherever they go._
>
> **Short-Lived.** Smallfolk mature at a similar pace to humans and are considered adults at the age of 20. On average, they live about 70 years.
>
> **Small Figures.** Smallfolk stand between 0.6 and 1.2 meters tall, weighing around 30 kilograms on average. Their light, compact builds make them naturally agile and nimble. Your [[Creature Size]] is Small. Because of your size, your [[Agility]] score increases by 2, and your [[Strength]] score decreases by 2.
>
> **Slow Stride.** Your [[Walking Speed]] is 6 meters.

> [!info] **[[Human Ancestry]]**
> _Humans are a versatile and ambitious people, known for their adaptability and drive to shape the world around them. Though their lives are shorter than many other ancestries, humans settle in nearly every corner of the realms, building cities, forging alliances, and leaving their mark on history._
>
> **Moderate-Lived.** Humans mature at the same pace as their peers and are considered adults at around the age of 18. On average, they live about 80 years.
>
> **Average Build.** Humans stand between 1.4 and 2.1 meters tall, weighing around 70 kilograms on average. Their builds are balanced, reflecting a blend of strength and agility. Your [[Creature Size]] is Medium.
>
> **Standard Stride.** Your [[Walking Speed]] is 7 meters.

### Select Additional Ancestry

You may optionally choose an Additional Ancestry to represent mixed heritage, allowing your character to draw influence from more than one lineage.

If you choose a [[Ancestry|Common Ancestry]] as your Additional Ancestry, select [[Creature Size]] and [[Speed|Walking Speed]] from that same Common Ancestry. Your character’s age and physical appearance may be freely blended between both ancestries.

If you select an [[Ancestry|Rare Ancestry]] as your Additional Ancestry, it modifies your [[Ancestry|Common Ancestry]]. Each Rare Ancestry provides specific guidance on how your ancestral traits change, replacing or altering aspects of your Common Ancestry as described. Current options for the [[Ancestry|Rare Ancestries]] are listed below:

> [!info] **[[Celestial Ancestry]]**\
> _As deities once found mortal partners and passed on part of their essence, rare divine blood now flows through your veins. This heritage makes you stand out, for better or for worse, shaping your mind toward nobler thoughts and extending your lifespan. A faint divine aura surrounds you, your eyes glimmer subtly, and your voice carries an unnatural clarity._
>
> **Divine Heritage.** Increases your [[Charisma]] score by 1 and decreases your [[Awareness]] score by 1.
>
> **Radiant Presence.** You emit a faint divine aura, your eyes glimmer subtly, and your voice carries an unnatural clarity.

> [!info] **[[Infernal Ancestry]]**\
> _The corruption has warped your bloodline with unnatural power. Vein-like patterns creep across your skin, and your eyes glow with a faint purple hue. This infernal taint grants unsettling advantages while driving you toward chaos and eroding your inner discipline. It also shortens your lifespan, slowly consuming you from within._
>
> **Chaotic Influence.** Your [[Awareness]] increases by 1, and your [[Intelligence]] decreases by 1.
>
> **Infernal Markings.** Vein-like patterns trace your skin, and your eyes glow with a faint purple light, reflecting the corruption within.

> [!info] **[[Giant Ancestry]]**\
> _The blood of giants flows through your lineage, shaping your body into a larger and more imposing form. Your frame grows heavier and more powerful, marked by thickened bones and broadened features that echo your colossal heritage._
>
> **Colossal Build.** Your [[Creature Size]] increases from [[Small]] to [[Medium]] or from [[Medium]] to [[Large]].
>
> **Mighty Strength.** Your [[Strength]] increases by 2, while your [[Agility]] decreases by 2.

These abilities represent the inherited traits of your [[Ancestry]], whether drawn from a [[Ancestry|Common Ancestry]] or an [[Ancestry|Rare Ancestry]]. Select the Talents and record them on your [[Character Sheet]].

## Select Background

Choosing a [[Background]] answers the question: _What did your character do before beginning their adventuring career?_ Your character may have been a [[Blacksmith]] or a [[Merchant]]. This choice grants [[Rank|Ranks]] in certain [[General Skill|General Skills]] and [[Expert Skill|Expert Skills]], one [[Talent]], improvements to two of your [[Ability Score|Ability Scores]], and may also provide additional [[Language|Languages]], [[Equipment]], and [[Money]]. You can either select a premade background or work with your [[Game Master]] to create a custom one.

**Talents:** [[Keen Mind]]
**Equipment:** [[Scribe’s Supplies]], Rare [[Tome]] related to your Expert Skill

### Custom Background

A custom background follows the same structure as a premade background. Work with your [[Game Master]] for guidance, or propose adjustments to an existing background to better fit your character’s theme.

In general, a background includes the following elements:

- **Ability Scores:** Select two of the four specified [[Ability Score|Ability Scores]] to increase by +1.
- **Skills:** Choose from the provided [[General Skill]] and [[Expert Skill]] options.
- **Languages:** Gain one or more possible [[Language|Languages]].
- **Talent:** Choose one [[Talent]].
- **Initial Wealth:** Starting currency as defined by the background.
- **Equipment:** A small set of starting [[Equipment]].

## Select Skills

There are two types of Skills: [[General Skill|General Skills]] and [[Expert Skill|Expert Skills]]. Each [[Skill]] has a [[Rank]], which ranges from 0 to 4. The Rank reflects how well-trained a character is in that skill and contributes to the modifier applied to a [[Check]].

The modifier from Rank is calculated as **Rank × 2**. This is also shown in the table below:

| Rank | Modifier | Description |
| :--------: | :------: | :---------: |
|     0      |    +0    |  Familiar   |
|     1      |    +2    |   Learned   |
|     2      |    +4    |  Practiced  |
|     3      |    +6    |   Expert    |
|     4      |    +8    |   Master    |

When creating a character, you gain [[Rank|Ranks]] for your [[Skill|Skills]]. In addition to the Ranks granted by your [[Background]], choose one of the following options:

- Gain 1 Rank in an [[Expert Skill]] and 1 Rank in a [[General Skill]].
- Gain 3 Ranks distributed among different [[General Skill|General Skills]].

### General Skills

These are [[General Skill|General Skills]] already listed on your [[Character Sheet]]. They represent common activities that most adventurers can attempt. While practice improves effectiveness, lack of training does not prevent a character from trying.

After assigning [[Rank|Ranks]], calculate the [[Skill Modifier]] for each [[General Skill]] and record it on your [[Character Sheet]].

> [!info] Calculating General Skill Modifier\
> General Skill Modifier = [[Ability Score]] + ([[Rank]] × 2)

Each [[General Skill]] has a default [[Ability Score]] that is added when calculating its modifier. In some cases, a [[General Skill]] can be used with a different [[Ability Score]] depending on the situation.

### Expert Skills

[[Expert Skill|Expert Skills]] represent highly specialized abilities, such as [[Lockpicking]] or [[Musicality]], which are difficult to attempt without proper training. Calculating Expert Skills differs from [[General Skill|General Skills]]: when making a check, you only add the Rank bonus to the Expert Skill modifier, as shown below:

> [!info] Calculating Expert Skill Modifier\
> Expert Skill Modifier = [[Rank]] × 2

These [[Expert Skill|Expert Skills]] are recorded on your [[Character Sheet]] in the designated space for Expert Skills. You cannot use any Expert Skills that are not listed on your Character Sheet.

Using an [[Expert Skill|Expert Skill]] differs slightly from using a [[General Skill|General Skill]]. If your character has an Expert Skill modifier in [[Smithing]], you can apply it to any [[Check]] related to that specialty. For example:

- **Crafting a weapon:** [[Crafting]] modifier + [[Smithing]] modifier
- **Recalling lore about a mystic weapon:** [[History]] modifier + [[Smithing]] modifier
- **Appraising the value of a weapon:** [[Investigation]] modifier + [[Smithing]] modifier

## Select Talents

Choosing [[Talent|Talents]] is one of the most important parts of character creation. After selecting your [[Ancestry Talent|Ancestral Talents]], you have 15 [[Legend Point|LP]] remaining. These points can be used to acquire any other [[Talent|Talents]] for which your character meets the requirements. If you want you can leave remaining [[Legend Point|LP]] and mark it in your [[Character Sheet]].

Below is an example of a [[Talent]]:

> [!note] **[[Vampiric Bite]]**
> **Requirement**: [[Mark of Bite]]
> **Cost**: 1 Legend Point
>
> _You have learned to siphon vitality from living creatures, turning their essence into your own strength._
>

- **Requirement:** Could be another [[Talent]], a specific [[Level]], [[Ability Score]], [[Rank]], or other condition.
- **Cost:** The number of [[Legend Point|Legend Points]] required to acquire the Talent.
- **Description:** A brief flavour text followed by the mechanical effect of the Talent.

Talents are grouped by relevance to make them easier to find. Some Talents are organized into [[Talent Tree|Talent Trees]], which show how Talents are connected and which Talents lead to others. An example of this structure can be seen in the Following Talents section at the end.

### Ancestry Talents

[[Ancestry Talent|Ancestral Talents]] require your character to have the matching ancestry. They provide thematic abilities and features that reflect your character’s lineage.

### General Talents

[[General Talent|General Talents]] are common and straightforward. They allow you to acquire additional Hit Points, learn new skills, or improve your Saving Throws.

### Exploration Talents

[[Adventuring Talent]] represent abilities developed during adventuring and exploration, helping characters navigate, survive, and interact with the world.

### Social Talents

[[Social Talent]] focus on persuasion, deception, diplomacy, and other skills for navigating social interactions.

### Martial Talents

[[Martial Talent]] reflect a character’s combat prowess, from mastering weapons to harnessing inner energy and unleashing devastating attacks. These Talents help warriors excel on the battlefield and adapt to any fight.

- [[Weapon Mastery Talent Tree]]
- [[Defence Talent Tree]]
- [[Ki Talent Tree]]
- [[Berserker Talent Tree]]

### Arcane Talents

[[Arcane Talent]] represent a character’s mastery of magic, combining study and arcane discipline. There is a General Talent Tree for abilities useful to any arcane scholar. Each special study also has its own Talent Tree.

- [[Arcane Talent Tree]]
- [[Spellblade Talent Tree]]
- [[Warding Talent Tree]]

### Divine Talents

[[Divine Talent]] are powers granted by higher powers. They provide strength and abilities for followers and champions to protect and defend others. There is a General Talent Tree for abilities useful to any follower, and each deity has its own Talent Tree for unique Talents tied to their domain.

- [[Divine Talent Tree]]
- [[Knowledge Talent Tree]]
- [[Light Talent Tree]]

### Innate Talents

[[Innate Talent]] reflect powers that are inherent or arise spontaneously, tied to a character’s nature, heritage, or chaotic magical force.

- [[Vampire Talent Tree]]
- [[Wild Magic Talent Tree]]

### Pact Talents

[[Pact Talent]] are powers gained through bonds with otherworldly entities or magical pacts. They grant unique abilities and enhancements to those who strike deals with forces beyond them.

- [[Draconic Pact Talent Tree]]
- [[Hexcore Talent Tree]]

### Resonant Talents

[[Resonant Talent]] represent abilities that harmonize with the world through music, movement, or other expressive forms. These Talents allow characters to influence, inspire, and shape their surroundings.

- [[Art of Melody Talent Tree]]
- [[Art of Motion Talent Tree]]

### Spiritual Talents

[[Spiritual Talent]] reflect a character’s connection to the unseen spiritual world. They allow characters to channel spiritual energy, commune with spirits, and wield spiritual forces. There is a General Talent Tree for any practitioner, plus specialty Talent Trees for unique abilities.

- [[Spiritual Talent Tree]]
- [[Nature Talent Tree]]
- [[Elemental Talent Tree]]

### Spellcasting Talents

[[Spellcasting Talent]] are general Talents that enhance a character’s spellcasting abilities, making their magic more potent, versatile, and effective.
