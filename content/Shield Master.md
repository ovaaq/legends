**Requirements**:: [[Shield Training]]
**Cost**:: 1 LP

_Mastery turns a shield into a formidable asset on the battlefield._

You use shields not just for protection, but also for offense. While wielding a shield, you gain the following benefits.

**Shield Strike.** As an [[Action]], you can make an [[Attack]] with your [[Shield]]. On a hit, you can either push the target [[Creature]] 1 metre or deal 1d4 [[Bludgeoning Damage]].

**Deflection.** If you aren't [[Incapacitated]], you use your [[Reaction]] to add your [[Shield|Shield's]] [[Armour Bonus]] bonus to any [[Agility Saving Throw]] you make against a harmful effect.
