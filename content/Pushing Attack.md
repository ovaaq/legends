_[[1st Level Martial Technique]]_

**Execution**:: [[Free Action]]\*
**Stamina**:: 1
**Range**:: Melee
**Weapon**:: [[Heavy]] or [[Polearm]]
**Duration**:: Instant

_\*When your [[Attack]] hits a [[Creature]]._

**Effect**:: You attempt to push the target away from you. The target must make a [[Strength]] [[Saving Throw]]. [[Creature|Creatures]] larger than you have [[Advantage]] on this [[Saving Throw]].

**Critical Success:** No effect.\
**Success:** The target is pushed 1 metre directly away from you.
**Failure:** The target is pushed 2 metres directly away from you.
**Critical Failure:** The target is pushed 4 metres directly away from you.

If the target's movement is interrupted by an obstacle, such as a wall, it takes [[Bludgeoning Damage]] equal to your [[Strength]].

**Type**:: [[Offensive Technique]]
