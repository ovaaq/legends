_[[1st Level Rune]]_

**Item**:: [[Shield]]

The [[Shield]] has 4 charges. While holding it, the wielder can use a [[Reaction]] immediately after being hit by a [[Melee Attack]] to expend 1 of the shield's charges and push the attacker up to 3 metres away. The shield regains 1d4 expended charges daily at [[Dawn]].
