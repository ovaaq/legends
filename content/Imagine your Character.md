Start by thinking about the core concept of your character. What are their main strengths and weaknesses? What kind of environment did they grow up in? What kind of tactics do you want to use during play?

In **Legends RPG**, it's usually easier to find rules that support your vision once you have a starting point. That starting point can be as simple as:

- _It would be cool to play a mage in full armour._
- _I want to be a holy person who was bitten by a vampire._
