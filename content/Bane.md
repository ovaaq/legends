_[[1st Level Spell]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: F, V, S
**Mana**:: 1
**Range**:: 20 metres
**Duration**:: [[Concentration]], 1 minute

**Effect**:: Up to three [Creatures](Creature.md) of your choice that you can see within range make a [[Charisma Saving Throw]].

**Critical Success:** No effect.
**Success:** The target's next [[Attack Roll]] or [[Saving Throw]] takes a 1d4 penalty.
**Failure:** The target gains 1d4 Bane Dice.
**Critical Failure:** The target gains 1d6 Bane Dice.

Whenever a target with Bane Die must make an [[Attack Roll]] or [[Saving Throw]], the target must subtract the Bane Dice from the [[Attack Roll]] or [[Saving Throw]].

**Upcasting**:: For each 1 additional mana spent, you can target one additional [[Creature]].

**Type**:: [[Enchantment Magic]]
