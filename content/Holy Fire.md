_[[Material]]_

As a [[Double Action]] you can throw a flask of [[Holy Fire]]. Target one [[Undead]] or [[Fiend]] [[Creature]] you can see within 4 metres of yourself. The target must make a [[Agility Saving Throw]] against DC 15.

**Critical Success:** No effect.
**Success:** The target takes half damage.
**Failure:** The target takes 2d10 [[Radiant Damage]].
**Critical Failure:** The target takes double damage.
