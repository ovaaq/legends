[[Karat]] is a unit of [[Gemstone]] size. A [[Gemstone|Gemstone's]] [[Karat]] determines its relative size. Some [[Spell|Spells]] require [[Gemstone|Gemstones]] as [[Material Component|Material Components]], making them valuable and sought-after goods.

|Karat|Size|
|---|---|
|1|Tiny|
|2|Small|
|3|Medium|
|4|Large|
|5|Huge|

Grinding each [[Karat]] of a [[Gemstone]] produces 2 grams of [[Gemstone Dust]].
