Divine Talents are powers granted by deities. They provide strength and abilities for followers and champions to protect and defend others. Each follower begins in [[Divine Talent Tree]] for abilities useful to any follower, and afterwards you can dedicate yourself to any domain listed in Sub Talent Trees.
