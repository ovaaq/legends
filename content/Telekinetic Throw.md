*[[Cantrip]]*

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: F, V, S, M*
**Mana**:: 0

**Range**:: 10 metres
**Duration**:: Instantaneous

*\*Small Object*

**Effect**:: You seize a loose object and hurl it at a creature within range. Make a [[Ranged Spell Attack]] against the target. On a hit, the target takes 1d10 [[Bludgeoning Damage]], [[Slashing Damage]] or [[Piercing Damage]] depending on the object.

**Upcasting**:: For each additional mana spent, the damage increases by 1d6. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 3 extra mana.
