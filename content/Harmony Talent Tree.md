Active, flexible abilities fueled by **Faith Points**.

The character actively channels harmony where it is needed.

- **Shared Burden**: Spend Faith to divide damage between yourself and another Creature.
- **Equal Measure**: Spend Faith to redistribute healing between nearby Creatures.
- **Restore Balance**: Spend Faith to reduce or remove a harmful condition.
- **Resonance**: When you spend Faith to benefit another Creature, gain a smaller benefit yourself.
- **Harmonic Pulse**: Spend Faith to grant several nearby allies a defensive bonus.
- **Perfect Harmony**: Spend Faith to temporarily strengthen beneficial effects shared between allies.

**Identity:** _I actively manipulate the balance between us._

Powerful abilities that can be used **once per day** or have limited daily uses.

These should feel considerably stronger than Faith abilities because you cannot repeatedly fuel them.

- **Blessing of Unity**: Several allies share a powerful defensive benefit.
- **Blessing of Peace**: Suppress aggression or hostility in an area.
- **Blessing of Fellowship**: Allies near you gain bonuses while remaining together.
- **Divine Intervention**: Turn a devastating hit against an ally into a miss or greatly reduce it.
- **Perfect Accord**: For a short duration, beneficial effects applied to one ally spread to another.
- **Balance Restored**: Remove several harmful effects from an ally at once.
- **Circle of Harmony**: Create an area where allies become increasingly difficult to overwhelm.

**Identity:** _At the critical moment, I invoke a major blessing._

Always-active abilities that require **no Faith Points and no daily use**.

This is where I would put the talents that make the Harmony character naturally better when cooperating with others.

- **United Front**: Gain a defensive benefit while near an ally.
- **Shared Strength**: Healing you receive also benefits another Creature.
- **Inner Peace**: Gain resistance against Fear, Charm, or similar mental effects.
- **Reciprocity**: When another Creature benefits you, your next beneficial effect on them is strengthened.
- **Strength in Unity**: Gain a benefit based on nearby allies.
- **No One Left Behind**: Gain increased movement when moving toward an endangered ally.
- **Harmonious Presence**: Nearby allies gain a small defensive benefit.
- **In Tune**: Gain a benefit against enemies your allies have already engaged.
- **Equilibrium**: Harmful effects against you are weakened while you are benefiting from one of your allies' effects.

**Identity:** _Simply fighting alongside me makes the group stronger._
