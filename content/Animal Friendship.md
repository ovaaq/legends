_[[1st Level Spell]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: F, V, S
**Mana**:: 1
**Range**:: 20 metres
**Duration**:: 24 hours

**Effect**:: Target a [[Natural Beast]] you can see within range. The target must succeed on a [[Charisma Saving Throw]] or gain the [[Charmed]] condition for the duration. The spell ends early if you or any of your allies deal damage to the target.

While the target is [[Charmed]], you have [[Advantage]] on [[Animal Handling]] [[Check|Checks]] made to interact with it.

**Upcasting**:: For each additional mana spent, you can target one additional [[Natural Beast]]. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 3 extra mana.

**Type**:: [[Enchantment Magic]]
