_[[1st Level Spell]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: F, V, S
**Mana**:: 1
**Range**:: 20 metres
**Duration**:: 24 hours

**Effect**:: Target a [[Natural Beast]] you can see within range. The target must make a [[Charisma Saving Throw]]. It makes it with [[Advantage]] if you or your allies are fighting it.

**Success:** No effect.\
**Failure:** The target is [[Charmed]] condition for the duration.

While the target is [[Charmed]], you have [[Advantage]] on [[Animal Handling]] [[Check|Checks]] made to interact with it. The spell ends early if you or your allies damage the target.

**Upcasting**:: For each 1 additional mana spent, you can target one additional [[Natural Beast]].

**Type**:: [[Enchantment Magic]]
