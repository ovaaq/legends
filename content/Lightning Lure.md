*[[Cantrip]]*

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: F, V, S
**Mana**:: 0

**Range**:: 10 metres
**Duration**:: Instantaneous

**Effect**:: You create a lash of lightning energy that strikes at one creature within range. Make a [[Ranged Spell Attack]] against the target. On a hit, the target takes 1d8 [[Lightning Damage]] and is pulled 2 meters towards you if its [[Creature Size]] is [[Large]] or smaller. On a [[Critical Hit]], the target is pulled 4 meters, or 2 meters if its [[Creature Size]] is [[Large]].

**Upcasting**:: For each additional mana spent, the damage increases by 1d6. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 3 extra mana.
