**Requirements**:: [[Axeman]]
**Cost**:: 1 LP

\*In your hands, axes are exceptionally lethal.

When you deal damage with an [[Axe]], its weapon damage die increases by one tier:

- 1d4 becomes 1d6
- 1d6 becomes 1d8
- 1d8 becomes 1d10
- 1d10 becomes 1d12
- 1d12 becomes 3d4

This [[Talent]] can be taken once.
