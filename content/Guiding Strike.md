_[[Cantrip]]_

**Casting**:: [[Action]]
**Components**:: V, S, M\*
**Mana**:: 0

**Range**:: 4 metres
**Duration**:: 1 minute

_\*[[Weapon]]_

**Effect**:: You call upon arcane insight to guide the next aim. Select a weapon you can see within range. The next time this weapon is used to make a [[Melee Attack|Melee]] or [[Ranged Attack]] in the next 1 minute, creature makes the attack with [[Advantage]].

**Upcasting**:: For each additional mana spent, the [[Attack Modifier]] increases by 2. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 3 extra mana.
