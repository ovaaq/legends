**Requirements**:: [[Second Wind]]
**Cost**:: 3 LP

_Flavour text_.

If you are [[Bloodied]], you can use 1 [[Action]] to gain [[Temporary Hit Point|Temporary Hit Points]] equal to 5 + 3 × [[Constitution]]. While you have these [[Temporary Hit Point|Temporary Hit Points]], you gain [[Resistance]] to all [[Damage Type|Damage Types]]. When these [[Temporary Hit Point|Temporary Hit Points]] are reduced to 0, you gain [[Exhausted]] (1). Once you use this [[Talent]], you can’t use it again until you finish a [[Long Rest]].
