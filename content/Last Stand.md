_Flavour text_.

If you are [[Bloodied]], you can use 1 [[Action]] to gain [[Temporary Hit Point|Temporary Hit Points]] equal to 10 + 6 × [[Constitution]] for 10 minutes. When these [[Temporary Hit Point|Temporary Hit Points]] are reduced to 0, you gain [[Exhausted]] (1). Once you use this [[Talent]], you can’t use it again until you finish a [[Long Rest]].
