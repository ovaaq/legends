**Requirements**:: [[Second Wind]]
**Cost**:: 3 LP

_Flavour text_.

If you are [[Bloodied]], you can use 1 Action to gain [[Temporary Hit Point|Temporary Hit Points]] equal to 5 + 3 × your [[Constitution]] score. While you have these temporary hit points, you gain [[Resistance]] to all damage. When these temporary hit points are reduced to 0, you gain [[Exhausted]] (1). Once you use this talent, you can’t use it again until you finish a [[Long Rest]].
