**Requirements**:: [[Arcane Recovery]]
**Cost**:: 3 LP

_You have trained to channel your mana with precision._

Choose one [[Strand of Magic]] (Creation, Destruction, Preservation, or Transformation). Whenever you cast a spell from the chosen Strand, you may reduce its mana cost by 1, to a minimum of 1 mana. After completing a [[Long Rest]], you may choose a different [[Strand of Magic]] for this talent.
