Talent Groups are broad categories used to organize [[Talent Tree|Talent Trees]]. They help players quickly find [[Talent Tree|Talent Trees]] that align with their character's abilities, playstyle, or theme.

| Talent Group                                                                  | Description                                             |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| [[Arcane Talent.md\|Arcane Talent]]             | Study and manipulation of arcane magic.                 |
| [[Ancestry Talent.md\|Ancestry Talent]]         | Traits inherited from heritage.                         |
| [[Crafting Talent.md\|Crafting Talent]]         | Creation and modification of items.                     |
| [[Divine Talent.md\|Divine Talent]]             | Miracles and spellcasting granted through faith.        |
| [[General Talent.md\|General Talent]]           | Broad talents useful to many characters.                |
| [[Innate Talent.md\|Innate Talent]]             | Powers and spellcasting originating from the character. |
| [[Martial Talent.md\|Martial Talent]]           | Combat training and physical mastery.                   |
| [[Pact Talent.md\|Pact Talent]]                 | Powers granted by supernatural agreements.              |
| [[Spellcasting Talent.md\|Spellcasting Talent]] | General spellcasting talents.                           |
