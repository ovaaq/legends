**Requirements**:: [[Spiritual Connection]]
**Cost**:: 1 LP

_Flavour text_.

You gain 1 additional [[Spirit Point]].

**Soul Anchor.** You have bound a dying spirit that is unwilling to pass on. It inhabits your soul anchor, causing the object to become faintly animated. This may take the form of a skull with flames dancing in its eye sockets, an instrument that plays itself or deck of cards.

Your soul anchor serves as a [[Spellcasting Focus]] for your spells, functioning as either a one-handed [[Simple Focus]] or a two-handed [[Advanced Focus]]. In addition, you can cast [[Thaumaturgy]] at will through the soul anchor.
