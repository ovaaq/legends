_Flavour text_.

**Soul Anchor.** You have bound a dying spirit that is unwilling to pass on. It inhabits your soul anchor, causing the object to become faintly animated and seemingly possessed. This may take the form of a skull with flames dancing in its eye sockets, an instrument that plays itself, or another mundane object touched by the spirit's presence.

Your soul anchor serves as a [[Spellcasting Focus]] for your [[Spell|Spells]]. In addition, you can cast [[Thaumaturgy]] at will through the soul anchor.
