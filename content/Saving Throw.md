Saving throws are dice rolls representing a creature's attempt to “save” themselves from harm.

**Critical Success:** You achieve a critical success if your roll exceeds the Difficulty Class (DC) by 10 or more, or if you roll a natural 20 and the check succeeds.

**Success:** You succeed if your roll meets or exceeds the Difficulty Class (DC).

**Failure:** You fail if your roll is below the Difficulty Class (DC).

**Critical Failure:** You suffer a critical failure if your roll falls short of the DC by 10 or more, or if you roll a natural 1 and the check fails.
