**Requirements**:: [[Divine Acolyte]]
**Cost**:: 1 LP

*Flavour text*.

**Divine Gift: Luminous Light.** As an [[Action]], you can produce light at will. Your [[Holy Symbol]] starts shining a [[Bright Light]] for an 8-meter radius and [[Dim Light]] for an extra 8 meters. The light can be coloured as you like. You can continue the light indefinitely until you stop or become unconscious. It is not possible to dispel this light. This light penetrates any [[Magical Darkness]].

**Commandments of Light.** These are the commandments that every follower of Light must follow. Sacrilege leads to loss of powers if you do not confess and atone your disobedience in a way that pleases deity.

- You shall strive to banish darkness from this world and replace it with purest light.    
- You shall always shine your light on those who need it.
- You shall not kill innocent creatures.
