_You have mastered the fundamentals of weaponry. Your path to martial excellence begins._

Increase your [[Martial]] [[Rank]] by 1.

**Stamina.** Your [[Stamina Maximum]] equals your [[Constitution]]. You expend [[Stamina Point|Stamina Points]] to perform [[Martial Technique|Martial Techniques]] and regain all expended [[Stamina Point|Stamina Points]] when you complete a [[Short Rest]].

**Martial Techniques.** [[Martial Technique|Martial Techniques]] are specialised combat manoeuvres learned through training and experience. Each technique belongs to one of four categories: [[Offensive Technique|Offensive]], [[Defensive Technique|Defensive]], [[Control Technique|Control]], or [[Support Technique|Support]].

Select two [[Martial Technique|Martial Techniques]] from the following list and add them to your [[Learned Martial Techniques]].

- [[Parry]]
- [[Charging Strike]]
- [[Disarming Attack]]
- [[Withdrawing Strike]]
- [[Trip Attack]]
- [[Pushing Attack]]

**Perform Technique.** You can perform any of your [[Learned Martial Techniques]] by paying its [[Stamina Point]] cost. You can apply only one [[Martial Technique]] to each [[Attack]].
