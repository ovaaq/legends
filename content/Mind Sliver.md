*[[Cantrip]]*

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: F, V, S
**Mana**:: 0

**Range**:: 20 metres
**Duration**:: Instantaneous

**Effect**:: You fill targets mind with overstimulating force. Choose one target you can see within range. Each target makes [[Intelligence Saving Throw]]:

**Success:** Target takes no damage.
**Failure:** Target takes 1d6 [[Psychic Damage]] and subtract 1d4 from the next [[Saving Throw]] it makes before the end of your next turn.
**Critical Failure:** Target takes 1d6 [[Psychic Damage]] and is [[Confused]] until the end of your next turn.

**Upcasting**:: For each additional mana spent, the damage increases by 1d6. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 3 extra mana.
