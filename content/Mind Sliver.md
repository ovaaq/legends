_[[Cantrip]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: F, V, S
**Mana**:: 0
**Range**:: 20 metres
**Duration**:: Instant

**Effect**:: You fill targets mind with overstimulating force. Choose one [[Creature]] you can see within range. Each target makes [[Intelligence Saving Throw]].

**Success:** No effect.
**Failure:**  A target takes takes 1d6 [[Psychic Damage]] and subtracts 1d4 from the next [[Saving Throw]] it makes before the end of your next [[Turn]].
**Critical Failure:** A target takes takes 1d6 [[Psychic Damage]] and is [[Confused]] until the end of your next turn.

**Upcasting**:: For each additional mana spent, the damage increases by 1d6. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 3 extra mana.

**Type**:: [[Decay Magic]]
