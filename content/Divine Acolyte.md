_Flavour text_.

**Faith Points.** You have 2 Faith Points. You regain all expended Spirit Points when you finish a [[Long Rest]].

**Divine Prayer.** As an [[Double Action]] you can spend 1 Faith Point to pray upon divine power to influence the natural flow of existence. Choose one of the four aspects:

- **Creation:** You conjure a simple, non-magical item worth no more than 1 gp and weighing no more than 0.5 kg. Examples include a toy, a flower, a stone, or a scrap of cloth. You cannot create exact replicas of specific objects or items with intricate mechanisms.

- **Preservation:** You protect a non-magical object from harm. The object gains 20 [[Temporary Hit Point|Temporary Hit Points]] for 24 hours. While these temporary hit points remain, it resists scratches, dents, cracks, and minor impacts. Once the temporary hit points are depleted, the object becomes vulnerable again.

- **Transformation:** You alter the form of a non-magical object or creature in a minor way, affecting no more than 10 litres of volume. Examples include:
  - Turning wood into stone or stone into wood
  - Changing the colour or other simple properties of an item

- **Destruction:** You damage a non-magical object. The object loses up to 10 [[Hit Point|Hit Points]]. Small, fragile items may be destroyed outright, while larger items may be partially broken. This effect cannot directly harm creatures and cannot destroy magical properties.
