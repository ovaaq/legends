_[[Cantrip]]_

**Casting**:: [[Double Action]]
**Components**:: F, V, S
**Mana**:: 0
**Range**:: 40 metres
**Duration**:: Instant

**Effect**:: You shoot bolt of necromantic energy towards a [[Creature]] within range. Make a [[Ranged Spell Attack]] against the target. On a hit, the target takes 1d6 [[Necrotic Damage]]. If you score a [[Critical Hit]] the target gains [[Exhausted]]\(1) condition.

**Upcasting**:: For each additional mana spent, the damage increases by 1d6. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 3 extra mana.

**Type**:: [[Decay Magic]]
