_[[Camp Action]]_

**Duration**:: 2 hours
**Description**:: Tend to injuries and accelerate natural healing.

You treat [[Creature|Creature's]] wounds. Make a [[Medicine]] [[Check]] DC 15:

**Critical Success:** The target regains [[Hit Point|Hit Points]]  equal to 2d4 + [[Medicine]] [[Rank]].
**Success:** The target regains [[Hit Point|Hit Points]]  equal to 1d4 + [[Medicine]] [[Rank]].
**Failure:** No effect.
**Critical Failure:** Reduce target’s [[Hit Point|Hit Points]] by 1d4.

If you expend one use of a [[Healer's Kit]], treat your result as one tier higher.
