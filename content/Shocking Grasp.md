*[[Cantrip]]*

**Casting**:: [[Double Action]]
**Components**:: V, S,
**Mana**:: 0

**Range**:: Touch
**Duration**:: Instantaneous

**Effect**:: You channel magic, causing electricity to gather in your palm, crackling. Make a [[Touch Spell Attack]] against a target within reach. On a hit, the target takes 1d10 [[Lightning Damage]]. If you score a [[Critical Hit]], the target can't have [[Reaction|Reactions]] until the start of your next turn.

**Upcasting**:: For each additional mana spent, the damage increases by 1d6. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 3 extra mana.
