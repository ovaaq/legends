_Flavour text_.

You gain 2 additional [[Spirit Point|Spirit Points]].

**Ghostwalk.** While your [[Ghost Form]] is active, you can spend 1 [[Spirit Point]] as a [[Double Action]] to become [[Invisible]] and [[Incorporeal]] until the start of your next [[Turn]].

While [[Incorporeal]], you can pass through physical [[Object|Objects]] and [[Creature|Creatures]]. You cannot take [[Physical Damage]] or physically interact with [[Object|Objects]].
