**Major Explosion.** Everyone [[Creature]] within a 4-meter radius must make an [[Agility Saving Throw]] DC 14. On a [[Failure]], [[Creature]] takes 3d4 [[Fire damage]] or double damage on a [[Critical Failure]].

**Toxic Gases.** Everyone [[Creature]] within a 2-meter radius must make an [[Constitution Saving Throw]] DC 14. On a [[Failure]], [[Creature]] takes 1d10 [[Poison damage]] or double damage on a [[Critical Failure]].

**Accidental Fire.** Make [[Agility Saving Throw]] DC 14. On a [[Failure]], you take 3d4 [[Fire damage]] or double damage on a [[Critical Failure]].

**Minor Explosion.** Everyone [[Creature]] within a 2-meter radius must make an [[Agility Saving Throw]] DC 14. On a [[Failure]], [[Creature]] takes 2d4 [[Fire damage]] or double damage on a [[Critical Failure]].

**Frailty.** Make [[Constitution Saving Throw]] DC 14. On a failed save, you have [[Vulnerability]] to all [[Damage Type|Damage Types]] until your next [[Long Rest]].

**Acid Splash.** Make [[Agility Saving Throw]] DC 14. On a [[Failure]], you take 1d6 [[Acid damage]] or double damage on a [[Critical Failure]].

**Nausea.** Make [[Constitution Saving Throw]] DC 14. On a failed save, you are [[Poisoned]] until you finish a [[Short Rest]].

**Headache.** Make [[Constitution Saving Throw]] DC 14. On a failed save, you have [[Disadvantage]] on any [[Check]] made with [[Intelligence]] or [[Awareness]] until you finish a [[Short Rest]].

**Strange Fumes.** Make [[Constitution Saving Throw]] DC 14. On a failed save, you fall [[Prone]] in an uncontrollable laughter for 1 minute.

**Smoke.** The mixture emits a cloud of thick smoke that creates a heavily obscured area in a 4-meter radius. A moderate wind (at least 5 m/s) disperses the smoke in 4 rounds; a strong wind (10 m/s or more) disperses it in 1 round.

**Unexpected Result.** You accidentally create a strange potion. Roll once on the [[Experimental Elixir]] table. (Roll using the list of the Talent you have taken, or the first one if you haven't chosen one).

**Transformation.** Your ingredients completely transmute into 1kg of pure [[Gold]].
