_Giant lore._

**Increased Size.** Your [[Creature Size]] increases by one category, such as from [[Small]] to [[Medium]] or from [[Medium]] to [[Large]]. This increase also affects your [[Attribute|Attributes]]. Your [[Strength]] increases by 2, your [[Constitution]] increases by 1, your [[Precision]] decreases by 1, and your [[Agility]] decreases by 2. In addition, your [[Walking Speed]] increases by 1 metre. Your [[Strength]] and [[Constitution]] maximums increase by the same amount.
