**Requirements**:: [[Blood Siphon]]
**Cost**:: 1 LP

_You don’t just cut them. You teach their blood how to escape._

**Empowerment.** Your Blood Dice become d12 instead of d10.

**Blood Curse**. When you hit a creature with a [[Melee Attack]], you may expend 1 Blood Die to invoke a Blood Curse. The target must attempt a [[Constitution Saving Throw]].

**Critical Success:** The creature is immune to Blood Curse for 24 hours.
**Success:** No effect.
**Failure:** The creature gains [[Bleeding]] (3).
**Critical Failure:** The creature gains [[Bleeding]] (3) and is [[Stunned]] for 1 round.
