**You Can See the Target, but the Target is Blinded**
When you attack a target you can see and the target is [[Blinded]], resolve the attack normally. The target suffers a −5 penalty to [[Evasion Class]] against the attack.

**You Cannot Locate the Target and the Target is Blinded**
When you attack a target whose location you do not know, roll 1d6. On a 4-6, you correctly locate the target. On a 1-3, the attack misses or strikes another creature determined by the GM.

If you locate the target, make the attack roll with [[Disadvantage]] against the target's [[Evasion Class]] with a −5 penalty.

**You Cannot Locate the Target and the Target is Not Blinded**
When you attack a target whose location you do not know, roll 1d6. On a 4-6, you correctly locate the target. On a 1-3, the attack misses or strikes another creature determined by the GM.

If you locate the target, make the attack roll with [[Disadvantage]]. The target's defences are otherwise unaffected.
