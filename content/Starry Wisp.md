_[[Cantrip]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: F, V, S
**Mana**:: 0

**Range**:: 40 metres
**Duration**:: Instantaneous

**Effect**:: You hurl a mote of star light at a creature within range. Make a [[Ranged Spell Attack]] against the target. On a hit, the target takes 1d10 [[Radiant Damage]].

**Upcasting**:: For each additional mana spent, the damage increases by 1d6. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 3 extra mana.
