**Burning (X).** A [[Burning]] [[Creature]] is on fire. At the start of each of its [[Turn|Turns]], it takes X Damage. If it gains [[Burning]] again, add the new Burning value to X, unless the effect states otherwise. Burning ends if a [[Creature]] uses a [[Double Action]] to extinguish the flames, the Burning [[Creature]] takes [[Cold Damage]], or it becomes wet.
