_[[2nd Level Spell]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: F, V, S
**Mana**:: 2
**Range**:: Self
**Duration**:: 24 hours

**Upcasting**:: For each additional mana spent, your [[Rank]] in the chosen [[Skill]] increases by 1. You can spend a maximum of 3 additional mana on this spell.
