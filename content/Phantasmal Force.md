_[[2nd Level Spell]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: F, V, S
**Mana**:: 2
**Range**:: 20 metres
**Duration**:: [[Concentration]], 1 minute

**Effect**:: You weave a phantasm into the mind of a creature you can see within range. The target makes an [[Intelligence Saving Throw]]. On a failure, it perceives a phantasmal creature, object, or phenomenon no larger than a 2-metre [[Cube]] for the duration. The phantasm is visible only to the target and can include sounds, temperature, and other sensory details.

The target treats the phantasm as real and rationalizes any contradictions caused by interacting with it.

As an [[Double Action]], the target can examine the phantasm with an [[Investigation]] [[Check]] against your [[Spell Save DC]]. On a success, the target recognizes the illusion and the spell ends.

If the phantasm depicts a harmful creature or hazard, it can deal 2d8 [[Psychic Damage]] to the target on each of your turns while the target is within the phantasm or within 1 metre of it. The target perceives this damage as originating from the illusion.

**Type**:: [[Enchantment Magic]]
