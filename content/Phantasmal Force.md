_[[2nd Level Spell]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: F, V, S
**Mana**:: 2
**Range**:: 20 metres
**Duration**:: [[Concentration]], 1 minute

**Effect**:: You weave a phantasm into the mind of a creature you can see within range. The target makes an [[Intelligence Saving Throw]].

On a failure, the target perceives a phantasm no larger than a 2-metre [[Cube]] for the duration. Only the target can perceive it. The phantasm can include any sensory details, and the target treats it as real, rationalizing any contradictions.

As an [[Double Action]], the target can examine the phantasm with an [[Investigation]] [[Check]] against your [[Spell Save DC]]. On a success, it recognizes the illusion and the spell ends.

If the phantasm depicts a harmful [[Creature]] or hazard, it deals 2d6 [[Psychic Damage]] to the target on each of your [[Turn|Turns]] while the target is within or adjacent to it. The target perceives this damage as coming from the phantasm.

**Type**:: [[Enchantment Magic]]
