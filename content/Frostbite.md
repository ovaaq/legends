_[[Cantrip]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: F, V, S
**Mana**:: 0

**Range**:: 20 metres
**Duration**:: Instantaneous

**Effect**:: You freeze the air around your enemy. Choose one target you can see within range. Target makes [[Constitution Saving Throw]]:

**Success:** Target takes no damage.
**Failure:** Target takes 1d10 [[Cold Damage]].
**Critical Failure:** Target takes 1d10 [[Cold Damage]] and subtract 1d4 from the next [[Agility Saving Throw]] it makes before the end of your next turn.

**Upcasting**:: For each additional mana spent, the damage increases by 1d6. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 3 extra mana.
