_You have inexplicable luck that seems to kick in at just the right moment._

**Luck Points.** You have 3 [[Luck Point|Luck Points]]. You regain all expended [[Luck Point|Luck Points]] at [[Dawn]].

**Stroke of Luck.** When you make a [[Check]], you can spend 1 [[Luck Point]] to roll an additional d20 and use the highest result. You can choose to spend the [[Luck Point]] after rolling the die.
