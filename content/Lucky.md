**Cost**:: 4 LP

_You have inexplicable luck that seems to kick in at just the right moment._

You have 3 Luck Points. You regain expended Luck Points at [[Dawn]].

**Stroke of Luck.** When you make an [[Attack Roll]], [[Skill]] [[Check]], or [[Saving Throw]], you may spend 1 Luck Point to roll an additional d20 and use the highest result. You can choose to spend the Luck Point after rolling the die, but before the outcome is determined.

If more than one creature spends a Luck Point to influence the outcome of a roll, the points cancel each other out; no additional dice are rolled.
