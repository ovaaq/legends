_[[Adventuring Item]], [[Equipment]]_

_Lore._

**Weight**:: 1 Stack
**Cost**:: 5 sp

This jar contains 10 doses of grease. As an [[Double Action]], you can expend 1 dose to coat a 1-meter square surface.

**Hazard.** The affected area is considered [[Difficult Terrain]].

**Slippery.** Any [[Creature]] that enters the area for the first time on a [[Turn]] or begins their [[Turn]] in the area must succeed on a DC 10 [[Agility Saving Throw]] or fall [[Prone]]. On a [[Critical Failure]] it takes additionally 1d6 [[Bludgeoning Damage]].

**Flammable.** If the grease is exposed to fire, it ignites. Any [[Creature]] that enters the area for the first time on a [[Turn]] or begins their [[Turn]] takes 1d6 [[Fire Damage]].
