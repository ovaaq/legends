_[[Advanced Weapon]], [[Equipment]]_

_Thrown with a tight spin, it whirls around the enemy's legs and hits with a heavy thump._

**Weight**:: 1 kg
**Cost**:: 2 gp
**Category**:: [[Snare]]
**Properties**:: [[Light]]. [[Finesse]], [[Special]], [[Thrown]], [[Ranged]] (10m / 20m)
**Damage**::

Attacks with this weapon target only the [[Creature|Creature's]] [[Evasion Class]]. On a hit, a [[Medium]] or smaller creature becomes [[Restrained]]. [[Formless]] [[Creature|Creatures]] are [[Immune]] to this effect.

**Escape.** A [[Restrained]] [[Creature]] can attempt an [[Athletics]] or [[Sleight of Hand]] [[Check]] DC 10 to escape.

**Destroy.** The weapon can be targeted directly to free the trapped creature. It has [[Armour Class]] 10 and [[Vulnerable]] to [[Slashing Damage]]. It is destroyed if it takes more than 5 [[Damage]].
