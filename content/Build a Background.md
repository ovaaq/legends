Your character's background describes the life they lived before becoming an adventurer. It determines their culture, education, training and resilience.

#### Culture

Choose the cultural region where your character was raised. Your culture shapes your worldview, customs, and upbringing.

#### Languages

All characters know [[Common]].

Choose one additional [[Language]] associated with your culture or another language your character had reason to learn through travel, work, study, or personal interest.

In addition, each [[Rank]] gained in [[Linguistic]] grants fluency in one additional [[Language]].

#### Saving Throws

Your past experiences have taught you how to endure hardship. Distribute 4 Ranks among your Saving Throws.
