_Flavour text_.

**Rune Prism.** You create a rune prism. Whenever you finish a [[Long Rest]], you can choose one [[1st Level Spell]] you can cast and imbue it into the prism. The spell remains stored in the prism until you finish your next [[Long Rest]]. While imbued, you can cast the spell through the prism without expending mana.

**Rune Binding.** You can imbue a [[Spell]] into a [[Gemstone]] weighing at least 10 grams per spell level, provided the [[Spell|Spell’s]] [[Strand of Magic]] matches the [[Gemstone]] as shown in the table below. Engraving the rune into the gemstone takes 1 hour per spell level, during which you must expend the spell’s required [[Mana]] and material components. Once imbued, the spell remains stored within the gemstone indefinitely. Any [[Creature]] holding the gemstone can activate it using the spell's normal action cost. Upon use, the gemstone loses its colour and vibrancy, turning into a dull, glass-like shard.
