**Petrified.** A [[Petrified]] [[Creature]] and its [[Non-magical]] [[Equipment]] are transformed into solid stone. It is [[Blinded]], [[Muted]], [[Deafened]], and [[Incapacitated]], and ceases aging. Its [[Armour Class]] becomes 16 and its [[Evasion Class]] becomes 5. It automatically fails [[Strength Saving Throw|Strength]] and [[Agility Saving Throw|Agility Saving Throws]] and is immune to [[Poison]] and [[Disease]]. Any [[Poison]] or [[Disease]] already affecting it is suspended until Petrified ends.
