**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: F, V, S, M\*
**Mana**:: 2
**Range**:: 20 metres
**Duration**:: Instant

_\*A 1-[[Karat]] [[Tourmaline]], consumed by the [[Spell]]._

**Effect**:: You animate a pile of bones or the corpse of a [[Small]] [[Creature]] within range. It becomes a [[Beast Skeleton]] if you chose bones, or a [[Zombie]] if you chose a corpse. You can control a number of these [[Undead]] equal to your [[Spellcasting]] [[Rank]].

On each of your [[Turn|Turns]], you can use a [[Double Action]] to mentally issue one command to all [[Undead]] you created with this [[Spell]] within 20 metres. Once given a command, the [[Undead]] continues to follow it until the task is complete. If given no command, it only defends itself against hostile [[Creature|Creatures]].

**Upcasting**:: For each 1 additional mana spent, the animated [[Undead]] gains 5 [[Hit Point Maximum|maximum Hit Points]] and a +1 bonus to [[Attack Roll|Attack Rolls]] and [[Damage Roll|Damage Rolls]].
