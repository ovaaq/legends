Your character's [[Ancestry]] defines their physical traits and lineage. It influences their appearance, lifespan, [[Creature Size]], [[Speed]], and grants access to [[Ancestry Talent|Ancestry Talents]].

### Choose Primary Ancestry

Every character must choose a Primary Ancestry. This ancestry represents the character's dominant heritage and determines their core ancestry traits. Your Primary Ancestry determines your character's Size, Speed, Lifespan, and other inherent ancestry features. Options are following:

### Additional Ancestry (Optional)

You may choose an Additional Ancestry to represent mixed heritage. This ancestry may be selected from either the Common Ancestries above or from the Rare Ancestries below.

An Additional Ancestry allows characters to represent lineages such as a Human with Celestial blood or a Dwarf-Giant hybrid. It also makes combinations such as a half-Dwarf, half-Elf character possible.

If you choose not to select an Additional Ancestry, you gain +1 [[Legend Point]] to spend when selecting [[Talent|Talents]].
