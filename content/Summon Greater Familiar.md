_[[3rd Level Spell]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: F, V, S, M\*
**Mana**:: 3
**Range**:: 2 metres
**Duration**:: Instant

_\*3 grams of [[Incense]] consumed by the spell._

**Effect**:: You summon a familiar in an unoccupied space within range, taking the form and statistics of a [[Natural Beast]] with [[Threat Level]] 1. Its creature type is [[Spirit]], and its [[Intelligence]] is -1.

While within 20 metres, you can communicate telepathically, and it can deliver your touch abilities using its [[Reaction]].

At 0 [[Hit Point]], the familiar disappears, leaving behind anything it carries. It returns when you cast [[Summon Familiar]] again. As an [[Action]], you can dismiss it to a pocket dimension or dismiss it permanently. While dismissed, you can use an [[Action]] to make it reappear within 10 metres.

Casting [[Summon Familiar]] again does not create another familiar. Instead, it changes its form.
