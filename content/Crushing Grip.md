_[[1st Level Martial technique]]_

**Execution**:: [[Free Action]]
**Stamina**:: 1
**Range**:: Melee
**Weapon**:: None
**Duration**:: 1 Minute

_\*When you successfully [[Grapple]] a target._

**Effect**:: At the start of each of the target’s [[Turn|Turns]], the target takes 1d6 + your [[Strength]] [[Bludgeoning Damage]]. The effect ends when the target is no longer [[Stuck]], [[Restrained]] or [[Immobilized]] by you.

**Type**:: [[Control Technique]]
