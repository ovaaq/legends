*[[Cantrip]]*

**Casting**:: [[Double Action]]
**Components**:: S
**Mana**:: 0

**Range**:: Melee
**Duration**:: Instantaneous

**Effect**:: You channel magic to cause your teeth or fingernails to sharpen, ready to deliver an attack. Make a [[Melee Spell Attack]] against the target.

On a hit, the target takes 1d8 [[Piercing Damage]] or [[Slashing Damage]] (your choice). If you score a [[Critical Hit]], the target gains [[Bleeding]](1) condition. 

**Upcasting**:: For each additional mana spent, the damage increases by 1d6. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 3 extra mana.
