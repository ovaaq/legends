*[[Cantrip]]*

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: V, S
**Mana**:: 0

**Range**:: 20 metres
**Duration**:: Instantaneous

**Effect**:: You send a spectral skeletal hand toward a creature within range.. Make a [[Ranged Spell Attack]] against the target. On a hit, the target takes 1d10 [[Necrotic Damage]], and it can't regain hit points until the start of your next turn. Until then, the hand clings to the target.

**Upcasting**:: For each additional mana spent, the damage increases by 1d6. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 3 extra mana.
