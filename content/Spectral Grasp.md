_[[Cantrip]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: V, S
**Mana**:: 0
**Range**:: 20 metres
**Duration**:: Instant

**Effect**:: You send a spectral skeletal hand toward a [[Creature]] within range. Make a [[Ranged Spell Attack]] against the target. On a hit, the target takes 1d10 [[Necrotic Damage]], and it can't regain [[Hit Point|Hit Points]] until the start of your next [[Turn]]. Until then, the hand clings to the target.

**Upcasting**:: For each 1 additional mana spent, increase the damage by 1d6.

**Type**:: [[Decay Magic]]
