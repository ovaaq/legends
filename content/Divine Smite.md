_Flavour text_.

You gain 2 additional [[Faith Point|Faith Points]].

**Divine Smite.** As an [[Action]] when you hit with a [[Melee Weapon Attack]], spend up to a number of [[Faith Point|Faith Points]] equal to your [[Prayer]] [[Rank]]. The target takes an additional 1d8 [[Radiant Damage]] for each [[Faith Point]] spent. If the target is an [[Undead]] or [[Fiend]], it takes an additional 1d8 [[Radiant Damage]].
