**Blinded.** A [[Blinded]] [[Creature]] cannot see and automatically fails any [[Check]] that requires sight. [[Blinded]] does not prevent a [[Creature]] from perceiving its surroundings through [[Blindsight]], [[Tremorsense]], or similar senses.

While unable to see an attacker, the [[Creature]] suffers a −5 penalty to its [[Evasion Class]]. When making an [[Attack]] against a target it cannot see, it has [[Disadvantage]] on the [[Attack]] roll.

**Blinded.** Blinded [[Creature]] cannot see.

- Blinded creature cannot see. Does not affect on [[Blindsight]], [[Tremorsense]] or other such abilities.
- [[Evasion Class]] gets -5 penalty.
