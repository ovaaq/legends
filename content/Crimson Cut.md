_[[Cantrip]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: V, S
**Mana**:: 0

**Range**:: 10 metres
**Duration**:: Instantaneous

**Effect**:: You summon a wound in your enemy. Choose one target you can see within range. Target makes [[Constitution Saving Throw]]:

**Success:** Target takes no damage.
**Failure:** Target takes 1d8 [[Slashing Damage]].
**Critical Failure:** Target takes 1d8 [[Slashing Damage]] and gains [[Bleeding]]\(1) condition.

**Upcasting**:: For each additional mana spent, the damage increases by 1d6. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 3 extra mana.
