_[[1st Level Spell]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: F, V, S
**Mana**:: 1

**Range**:: Touch
**Duration**:: 8 hours

**Effect**:: You touch a willing [[Creature]] who isn’t wearing [[Armour]], and a protective magical force surrounds it until the spell ends. The [[Creature|Creature’s]] [[Armour Class]] becomes 14. The spell ends if the target dons [[Armour]].

**Upcasting**:: For each additional mana spent, [[Armour Class]] increases by 1. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 3 extra mana.
