_[[1st Level Spell]]_

**Casting**:: [[Action]]
**Components**:: F, V, S
**Mana**:: 1
**Range**:: Self
**Duration**:: 1 hour

**Effect**:: You bolster yourself with temporal life force. For the duration you gain 1d4 + 4 [[Temporary Hit Point|Temporary Hit Points]].

**Upcasting**:: For each additional mana spent, the amount of [[Temporary Hit Point|Temporary Hit Points]] increases by 1d4. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 3 extra mana.

**Type**:: [[Warding Magic]]
