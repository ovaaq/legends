_[[3rd Level Spell]]_

**Casting**:: [[Double Action]]
**Components**:: V, S
**Mana**:: 3
**Range**:: 20 metres
**Duration**:: Instant

**Effect**:: You teleport to an unoccupied space you can see within range. Before you arrive, a thunderous boom erupts. Each [[Creature]] in a 2-metre radius sphere centred on that point must make a [[Constitution Saving Throw]].

**Critical Success:** No effect.
**Success:** A target takes half damage.
**Failure:** A target takes takes 2d10 [[Lightning Damage]].
**Critical Failure:** A target takes double damage and is [[Deafened]] until the start of your next [[Turn]].

You can bring objects with you as long as their total weight does not exceed what you can carry. Additionally, you can teleport one creature of your size or smaller, along with its carried gear up to its [[Carrying Capacity]]. If the [[Creature]] is not [[Willing]], it must succeed on a [[Charisma Saving Throw]] against your [[Spell Save DC]] to not to be teleported. The [[Creature]] must be within 1 metre of you when you cast the [[Spell]], and there must be an unoccupied space within 1 metre of your destination for it to appear; otherwise, it remains behind.

**Upcasting**:: For each additional mana spent, the range increases by 5 metres. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 3 extra mana.

**Type**:: [[Distortion Magic]]
