_[[2nd Level Spell]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: F, S
**Mana**:: 2
**Range**:: Self
**Duration**:: Instant

**Effect**:: You conjure two magical fruits. Roll 1d6 for each fruit and consult the Magic Fruit table to determine which fruit appears. The fruits retain their magic for 8 hours, after which they disappear.

| d6  | Effect                                                                           |
| --- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **Goodberry.** 4 berries that Provides enough food and water for 1 day.          |
| 2   | **Starfruit.** Restores 4d6 [[Hit Point\|Hit Points]].                           |
| 3   | **Seedfruit.** Rapidly grow 5 meter tall tree.                                   |
| 4   | **Thornfruit.** If it breaks agi save or 2d8 [[Piercing Damage]] in 2 metre area |
| 5   |                                                                                  |
| 6   |                                                                                  |

**Upcasting**:: For each additional Mana spent, you conjure one additional fruit, rolling separately for each. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 3 additional Mana.
