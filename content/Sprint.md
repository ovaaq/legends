_[[Movement Action]]_

**Execution**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Target**:: Self

You can move an additional distance equal to three times your [[Walking Speed]] during this [[Turn]]. After taking this action, make a DC 15 [[Constitution Saving Throw]]. On a failure, you gain one level of the [[Temporally Exhausted]] condition. All levels of [[Temporally Exhausted]] are removed after 10 minutes of rest.
