_[[3rd Level Spell]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: F, V, S
**Mana**:: 3
**Range**:: 40 metres
**Duration**:: [[Concentration]], 10 minutes

**Effect**:: You create an illusion of an [[Object]], [[Creature]], or other visible phenomenon within range that fits inside a 4-metre [[Cube]]. The illusion can produce appropriate sounds, smells, and temperatures, but cannot cause damage or harmful effects.

As an [[Action]], you can move the illusion to another point within range and alter its appearance and sounds to make the movement appear natural.

Physical interaction reveals the illusion, as [[Object|Objects]] and [[Creature|Creatures]] pass through it. A [[Creature]] can also reveal it with a successful [[Investigation]] [[Check]] against your [[Spell Save DC]]. Once revealed, the illusion appears faint to that [[Creature]].

**Upcasting**:: For each 1 additional mana spent, increase the cube by 1 metre.

**Type**:: [[Projection Magic]]
