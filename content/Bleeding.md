**Bleeding (X).** A [[Bleeding]] [[Creature]] suffers ongoing blood loss. At the start of each of its [[Turn|Turns]], it takes X Damage. If it gains [[Bleeding]] again, add the new [[Bleeding]] value to X. [[Bleeding]] ends if the [[Creature]] receives healing or another [[Creature]] succeeds on a [[Medicine]] [[Check]] (DC = 12 + X) to treat the wound.
