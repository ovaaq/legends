_Flavour text_.

When you spend at least 1 hour engaged in continuous conversation with a [[Creature]] or closely observing them in a social setting, you automatically discern one of their core psychological traits (determined by the Game Master):

- **Drive:** What fundamentally motivates their current actions.
- **Trigger:** A specific subject, action, or stimulus that upsets or provokes them.
- **Flaw:** A significant personal weakness or blind spot in their character.
