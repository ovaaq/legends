_Is it green?_

**Cost**:: 1 gp
**Weight**:: 0.1 kg
**Duration**:: Instant

Oil of Confusion -You can use the poison in this vial to coat one slashing or piercing weapon or up to three pieces of ammunition. Applying the poison takes an action. Once applied, the poison retains potency for 1 minute before drying. A creature hit by the poisoned weapon or ammunition must make a DC 12 Intelligence saving throw or begin to hallucinate.
