**Type**:: [[Alchemical Item]]
**Weight**:: 0.1 kg

As a [[Double Action]], you throw the Alchemist's Frost, which shatters on impact and erupts into a 3-metre-diameter sphere. Each [[Creature]] in the area must make a DC 10 [[Constitution Saving Throw]].

**Critical Success:** No effect.
**Success:** A target takes half damage.
**Failure:** A target takes takes 1d12 [[Cold Damage]].
**Critical Failure:** A target takes double damage.

This freezes any [[Liquid]] [[Object|Objects]] in the area that aren't being worn or carried.
