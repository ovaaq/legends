_[[Cantrip]]_

**Casting**:: [[Double Action]]
**Components**:: V
**Mana**:: 0
**Range**:: 4 metres
**Duration**:: [[Concentration]], 10 minutes

**Effect**:: You attempt to influence the mind of a [[Humanoid]] within range that can see and understand you. The target must make a [[Charisma Saving Throw]].

**Success:** The target realizes you attempted to influence it.
**Failure:** You gain [[Advantage]] on all [[Check|Checks]] using [[Charisma]] against the target for the duration. When the effect ends, the target realizes it was affected by the spell.
**Critical Failure:** You gain [[Advantage]] on all [[Check|Checks]] using [[Charisma]] against the target for the duration.

**Type**:: [[Enchantment Magic]]
