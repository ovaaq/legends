_Fiendish lore._

**Shortened Lifespan.** Your fiendish blood takes a toll on your body, reducing your lifespan by one quarter compared to the average lifespan of your ancestry.

**Demonic Features.** You may have horns or tail. Your eyes may be red, purple, or blue. Scar-like veins may mark your skin, or your entire complexion may bear hues of those colours.
