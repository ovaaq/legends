_[[2nd Level Spell]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: F, V, S
**Mana**:: 2
**Range**:: 10 metres
**Duration**:: 10 minutes

**Effect**:: You weave a distracting string of words, causing [[Creature|Creatures]] of your choice that you can see within range and that can hear you to make a [[Charisma Saving Throw]]. If your companions are fighting a [[Creature]], it has [[Advantage]] on the [[Saving Throw]].

**Success:** No effect.
**Failure:** The target has [[Disadvantage]] on [[Perception]] [[Check|Checks]] made to perceive anything else than you for the duration.
**Critical Failure:** The target cannot take [[Perception]] [[Check|Checks]] for the duration.

The spell ends if you become incapacitated, if you can no longer speak, or if the target can no longer hear you.

**Type**:: [[Enchantment Magic]]
