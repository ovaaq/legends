_[[2nd Level Spell]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: F, V, S
**Mana**:: 2
**Range**:: 20 metres
**Duration**:: [[Concentration]], 1 minute

**Effect**:: Choose a [[Humanoid]] that you can see within range. The target must make a [[Charisma Saving Throw]].

**Critical Success:** No effect.
**Success:** The target is [[Restrained]] until the start of your [[Turn]].
**Failure:** The target is [[Immobilized]].
**Critical Failure:** The target is [[Immobilized]] and has [[Disadvantage]] on its first [[Saving Throw]] to end the [[Spell]].

At the end of each of its [[Turn|Turns]], the target repeats the [[Saving Throw]], ending the spell on a [[Success]].

**Upcasting**:: For each 2 additional mana spent, you can target one additional [[Humanoid]].

**Type**:: [[Enchantment Magic]]
