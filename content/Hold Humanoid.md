_[[2nd Level Spell]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: F, V, S
**Mana**:: 2
**Range**:: 20 metres
**Duration**:: [[Concentration]], 1 minute

**Effect**:: Choose a [[Humanoid]] that you can see within range. The target must make a [[Charisma Saving Throw]].

**Critical Success:** No effect.
**Success:** The target takes is [[Restrained]] until the start of your turn.
**Failure:** The target takes is [[Immobilized]].
**Critical Failure:** The target takes is [[Immobilized]] and has [[Disadvantage]] on resisting the effect.

At the end of each of its turns, the target can make another [[Charisma Saving Throw]]. On a success, the spell ends on the target.

**Upcasting**:: For each 2 additional mana spent, you can target one additional [[Humanoid]].

**Type**:: [[Enchantment Magic]]
