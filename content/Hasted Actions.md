**Cost**:: 3 LP

_Flavour._

**Haste Points.** You have 2 [[Haste Point|Haste Points]]. You regain all expended [[Haste Point|Haste Points]] when you finish a [[Long Rest]]. You can spend [[Haste Point|Haste Points]] to gain the following benefits.

**Explosive Start.** When you make an [[Initiative]] [[Check]], you can spend 1 [[Haste Point]] to reroll the [[Check]]. You must use the new result.

**Accelerated Reactions.** Once per [[Round]], you can spend 1 [[Haste Point]] to gain an additional [[Reaction]].

**Quick Resolve.** Once per [[Turn]], you can spend 1 [[Haste Point]] to reduce the action cost of a single action by 1, to a minimum of 1.
