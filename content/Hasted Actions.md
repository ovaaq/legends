**Cost**:: 3 LP

_Flavour._

You have 2 [[Haste Point|Haste Points]]. You regain all expended [[Haste Point|Haste Points]] when you finish a [[Long Rest]]. You can expend [[Haste Point|Haste Points]] to gain the following benefits:

**Explosive Start.** When you do [[Initiative]] [[Check]], you can expend 1 [[Haste Point]] to reroll the d20. You must use the new roll.

**Accelerated Reactions.** Once per round, you can spend 1 [[Haste Point]] to gain an additional [[Reaction]].

**Quick Resolve.** Once per turn, you can spend 1 [[Haste Point]] to reduce the action cost of a single action by 1 (to a minimum of 1 action).
