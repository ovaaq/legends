Sorcery measures connection to magical forces and the ability to harness them. Vital for spellcasters.

Characters with high [[Sorcery]] gain the following benefits:

- **Spell Attack:** Add Sorcery to [[Spell Attack|Spell Attacks]].
- **Spell:** Your [[Spell Save DC]] equals 8 + your [[Spellcasting Skill]] [[Rank]] + [[Sorcery]].
- **Mana:** The benefits of the [[Increased Mana]] [[Talent]] scale with your Sorcery, granting additional [[Mana]].
