Reflects connection to magical forces and the ability to harness them. Vital for spellcasters.

Characters with high Sorcery gain the following benefits:

- **Combat:** Add Sorcery to [[Spell Attack|Spell Attacks]]
- **Combat:** [[Spell Save DC]] equals 8 + [[Spellcasting Skill]] + [[Sorcery]]
- **Mana:** Starting [[Mana Maximum]] equals [[Sorcery]]
