Sorcery measures connection to magical forces and the ability to harness them. Vital for spellcasters that use [[Mana]].

Characters with high [[Sorcery]] gain the following benefits:

- **Spell Attack:** Add [[Sorcery]] to [[Spell Attack|Spell Attacks]].
- **Mana:** The benefits of the [[Increased Mana]] [[Talent]] scale with your [[Sorcery]].
