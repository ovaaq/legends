**Cost**:: 2 LP

_Flavour text_.

Increase [[Rank]] of [[Runecraft]] by 1.

Add these [[Rune|Runes]] to your [[Rune List]]:

##### 1st Level Runes

- [[Rune of Might]]
- [[Rune of Warding]]
- [[Rune of Strength]]
- [[Rune of Vitality]]

**Runic Knowledge.**  Select two [[1st Level Rune|1st Level Runes]] from your [[Rune List]] and add them to your [[Known Runes List]].

**Inscribe Rune.** By spending 2 hours of work and using 1 [[Mana]] per [[Rune]] level, you can carve a [[Rune]] from [[Known Runes List]] into a [[Non-magical]] [[Object]] that meets the rune’s item description. Upon completion, the [[Object]] becomes a [[Magic Item]] that requires [[Attunement]].

**Rune Capacity.** You can have only one active [[Rune]] at a time. Your runes remain on the item indefinitely, but if you die, they vanish after a number of days equal to your [[Sorcery]].

**[[Runecraft Save DC]]** = 8 + [[Runecraft]] [[Rank]] + [[Sorcery]]
