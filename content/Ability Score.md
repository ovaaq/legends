A numerical value representing one of your core attributes. Eight Ability Scores determine how well your character can perform various actions. For most [[Humanoid]] creatures, the baseline is 0, while adventurers typically range between -5 and +5. Ability Scores are divided into Physical and Mental groups, which are listed below.
