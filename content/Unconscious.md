**Unconscious.** An [[Unconscious]] [[Creature]] is [[Incapacitated]], unaware of its surroundings, and falls [[Prone]], dropping anything it is holding. Its [[Evasion Class]] becomes 5, [[Attack Roll|Attack Rolls]] against it have [[Advantage]], and it automatically fails [[Strength Saving Throw|Strength]] and [[Agility Saving Throw|Agility Saving Throws]].
