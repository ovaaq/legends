- An unconscious creature is [[Incapacitated]], can’t move or speak, and is unaware of its surroundings.
- The creature drops whatever it’s holding and falls [[Prone]].
- The creature automatically fails [[Strength Saving Throw|Strength]] and [[Agility Saving Throw|Agility Saving Throws]].
- [[Attack Roll|Attack Rolls]] against the creature have [[Advantage]].
