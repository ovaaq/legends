**Modifier**:: [[Agility]]

An **Acrobatics** [[Check]] covers your attempt to stay on your feet in a tricky situation, such as running across a sheet of ice, balancing on a tightrope, or keeping your footing on a rocking ship’s deck. An Acrobatics check may also be used to determine whether you can perform acrobatic stunts, including dives, rolls, somersaults, and flips.
