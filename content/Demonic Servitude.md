_Flavour text_.

Increase your [[Invocation]] [[Rank]] by 1.

**Demonic Mark.** Your patron’s influence visibly changes you, giving you horns, sharpened teeth, burning eyes, scaled skin, an unnatural shadow, or another demonic feature.

**Regeneration.** Whenever you complete a [[Long Rest]], you regain 5 additional [[Hit Point|Hit Points]] and gain 5 [[Temporary Hit Point|Temporary Hit Points]].

**Souls.** Your patron demands regular payment usually in form of souls of humanoids they feed on. Determine the nature of this payment with your [[Game Master]] when you gain this talent.

When any of your pact talents requires a [[Creature]] to make a [[Saving Throw]], it uses your [[Pact Save DC]].
