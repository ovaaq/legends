_[[2nd Level Spell]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: S, M\*
**Mana**:: 2
**Range**:: Self
**Duration**:: Instant

_\*[[Divination Tools]]._

**Effect**:: You divine a future you intend to bring about within the next 2 hours. The [[Game Master|GM]] chooses one omen.

- **Peril.** Significant danger lies ahead.
- **Fortune.** Opportunity for wealth awaits.
- **Calm.** Nothing noteworthy is likely to occur.
- **Revelation.** Knowledge could be uncovered.
- **Conflict.** Significant opposition awaits.

The omen reveals the most prominent outcome. Each casting after the first before your next [[Long Rest]] has a cumulative 25% chance to produce a random omen.

**Upcasting**:: For every 2 additional mana spent, you receive one additional omen. The [[Game Master|GM]] reveals them in order of prominence. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 4 extra mana.

**Type**:: [[Revelation Magic]]
