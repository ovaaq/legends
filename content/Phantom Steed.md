_[[3rd Level Spell]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: F, V, S
**Mana**:: 3
**Range**:: 10 metres
**Duration**:: 1 hour

**Effect**:: A [[Large]], ghostly horselike [[Creature]] appears in an unoccupied space on the ground within range. You determine its appearance.

The steed uses the statistics of a [[Horse]], except its [[Walking Speed]] is 20 metres. You and one other [[Creature]] can ride it.

The [[Spell]] ends if the steed takes damage or you dismiss it as an [[Action]]. When the [[Spell]] ends, the steed fades over 1 minute, allowing its riders to safely dismount.

**Upcasting**:: For each 1 additional mana spent, increase the duration by 1 hour.

**Type**:: [[Summon Magic]]
