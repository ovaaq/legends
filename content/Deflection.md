*[[Cantrip]]*

**Casting**:: [[Reaction]]*
**Components**:: V, S
**Mana**:: 0

**Range**:: Self
**Duration**:: Instantaneous

*\*Which you take when you are hit by an [[Attack]]*

**Effect**:: An invisible barrier of magical force surrounds you, protecting you from harm. Reduce the incoming damage by an amount equal to your [[Sorcery]] Score.

**Upcasting**:: For each additional mana spent, the damage increases by 1d6. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 3 extra mana.
