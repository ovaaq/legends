_[[1st Level Martial Technique]]_

**Execution**:: [[Reaction]]\*
**Stamina**:: 0
**Range**:: Self
**Weapon**:: None
**Duration**:: Instant

_\*When you get hit by an [[Attack]]._

**Effect**:: You fall [[Prone]] and feign death. Make a DC 13 [[Deception]] [[Check]]. On a success, [[Creature|Creatures]] that witnessed you fall believe you are dead. Moving or taking an [[Action]] ends the deception.

**Type**:: [[Defensive Technique]]
