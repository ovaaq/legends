**CAST TIME**: ▶▶▶
**MANA**: 0
**RANGE**: 12 meters
**SPELL PARTS**: F, V, S, M\*
**LEVEL**: [[1st Level Spell]]
**DURATION**: Instantaneous

**EFFECT**: You try to compel a [[Creature]] into a duel. One [[Creature]] that you can see within range makes a [[Charisma Saving Throw]]. On a failed save, the target has [[Disadvantage]] on [[Attack Roll|Attack Rolls]] against [[Creature|Creatures]] other than you, and it can't willingly move to a space that is more than 12 meters away from you.

The spell ends if you make an [[Attack Roll]] against a [[Creature]] other than the target, if you cast a spell on an enemy other than the target, if an ally of yours damages the target, or if you end your turn more than 12 meters away from the target.

**UPCASTING**: For each additional mana spent, the range and required distance increases by 12 meters. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 3 extra mana.
