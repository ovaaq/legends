_[[Potion]]_

_Make it big._

**Cost**:: 1 gp
**Weight**:: 0.1 kg
**Duration**:: 10 minutes

**Effect**:: Your [[Creature Size]] increases by one. For the duration, you gain +2 [[Strength]], +1 [[Constitution]], +5 [[Hit Point Maximum]], and +2 metres to your [[Walking Speed]], but suffer -2 [[Agility]] and -1 [[Precision]].

Your equipment and clothing do not grow with you. If there is not enough space for you to grow, you take 1d10 [[Bludgeoning Damage]] and stop growing. Fragile objects restricting your growth may break as you expand.
