_[[1st Level Spell]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: F, V, S
**Mana**:: 1
**Range**:: 20 metres
**Duration**:: 1 minute

**Effect**:: You ward a [[Creature]] within range. Any [[Creature]] that [[Attack|Attacks]] the warded [[Creature]] or targets it with a harmful Spell must first make a [[Charisma Saving Throw]].

**Critical Success:** No effect.
**Success:** The attack is made with [[Disadvantage]]. If it is a [[Saving Throw]], the warded creature has [[Advantage]] on that save.
**Failure:** The attacker must choose a different target or lose the [[Attack]] or [[Spell]].
**Critical Failure:** The attacker loses the [[Attack]] or [[Spell]].

This spell doesn’t protect the warded [[Creature]] from area effects. If the warded [[Creature]] makes an [[Attack]] or casts a harmful [[Spell]], this spell ends.

**Type**:: [[Warding Magic]]
