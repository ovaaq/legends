_[[Cantrip]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: F, V, S
**Mana**:: 0

**Range**:: 40 metres
**Duration**:: Instant

**Effect**:: A beam of crackling energy streaks toward a target within range. Make a [[Ranged Spell Attack]] against the target. On a hit, the target takes 1d10 [[Shadow Damage]].

**Upcasting**:: For each additional 2 mana spent, the amount of beams is increased by one. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 4 extra mana.
