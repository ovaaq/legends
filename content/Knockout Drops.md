_[[Uncommon]] [[Inhaled Poison]]_

_Is it green?_

**Execution**:: [[Double Action]]
**Cost**:: 1 gp
**Weight**:: 0.1 kg
**Duration**:: 2 Hours

As a [[Double Action]], you can pour the drops onto a cloth and press it over the nose and mouth of an adjacent [[Creature]], forcing it to make a DC 15 [[Constitution Saving Throw]].

**Critical Success:** No effect.
**Success:** The target becomes [[Dazed]] for 1 [[Turn]].
**Failure:** The target becomes [[Incapacitated]] until the start of your next [[Turn]]. You must use a [[Double Action]] on your next turn to force the repeat [[Saving Throw]].
**Critical Failure:** The target becomes [[Unconscious]] for 2 hours.

An [[Unconscious]] [[Creature]] wakes up if it takes more than 10 damage. Once awake, the [[Creature]] gains the [[Confused]] condition until its next [[Short Rest]].
