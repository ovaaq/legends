**Requirements**:: [[Arcane Spellcasting]]

_Using specially prepared inks and ancient incantations passed down by your wizardly order, you awaken an arcane sentience within your [[Spellbook]] that aid you._

**Arcane Assistant.** You can add 1d4 bonus to your [[Arcana]] [[Check|Checks]] while having your [[Spellbook]] within 1 metre.

**Emergency Preparation.** Once per [[Long Rest]], you can cast an unprepared arcane [[Spell]] from your [[Spellbook]].
