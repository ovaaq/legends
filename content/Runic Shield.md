_Flavour text_.

**Rune Shield.** Your maximum number of active [[Shield Rune|Shield Runes]] is 4. They glow with [[Dim Light]]. You regain all expended [[Shield Rune|Shield Runes]] after a [[Long Rest]].

**Absorb Harm.** Whenever you take [Damage](Damage) from an attack, hazard, or spell, you can use a [Reaction](Reaction) to expend any number of active [[Shield Rune|Shield Runes]], rolling 1d6 for each rune expended. You subtract the total from the damage dealt by the attack, hazard, or spell.
