**Requirements**:: [[Dwarven Ancestry]], [[Giant Ancestry]]
**Cost**:: 1 LP

_Flavour text_.

When you take damage, you can use your [[Reaction]] to roll a d12. Add your [[Constitution]] to the number rolled and reduce the damage by that total.

You can use this talent once, and you regain its use when you finish a [[Long Rest]].
