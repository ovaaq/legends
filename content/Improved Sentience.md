**Requirements**:: [[Sentient Spellbook]]
**Cost**:: 3 LP

_Flavour text_.

**Spell Reference.** When you cast a [[Spell]] of 1st level or higher that deals damage, you can replace its [[Damage Type]] with a [[Damage Type]] dealt by another [[Spell]] in your [[Spellbook]]. The referenced [[Spell]] must be the same level as the [[Spell]] you cast.

**Floating Grimoire.** As an [[Action]], you can command your [[Spellbook]] to float beside you. While floating, it follows you, remains within 1 metre of you, and opens to any page you request. The effect ends when you dismiss it, become unconscious, or the [[Spellbook]] is moved more than 1 metre from you. While floating within 1 metre of you, the [[Spellbook]] can serve as the [[Spellcasting Focus]] for your arcane [[Spell|Spells]], leaving your hands free.

**Spell sheet Protection.** When you take damage while your [[Spellbook]] is floating within 1 metre of you, you can use your [[Reaction]] to prevent all of the triggering damage. To do so, roll 1d6 and permanently erase [[Spell|Spells]] from your [[Spellbook]] whose combined levels equal or exceed the result. If you cannot, you cannot use this benefit.
