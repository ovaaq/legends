*[[1st Level Spell]]*

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: F, V, S
**Mana**:: 1

**Range**:: Self
**Duration**:: 1 hour

**Effect**:: Protective magical frost surrounds you. You gain 5 [[Temporary Hit Point|Temporary Hit Points]]. If a [[Creature]] hits you with a [[Melee Attack]] before the spell ends, the creature takes 5 [[Cold Damage]] damage. The spell ends early if you loose these [[Temporary Hit Point|Temporary Hit Points]].

**Upcasting**:: For each additional mana spent, the [[Temporary Hit Point|Temporary Hit Points]] and the [[Cold Damage]] damage both increase by 5. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 3 extra mana.
