_Flavour text_.

When you take the [[First Aid]] action and expend one use of a [[Healer's Kit]], replace the [[Success]] and [[Critical Success]] effects with the following effects. You attempt to stabilize a [[Dying]] [[Creature]]. Make a [[Medicine]] [[Check]] DC 10.

**Critical Success:** The target regains 2d4 + 2 [[Hit Point|Hit Points]].
**Success:** The target regains 1d4 + 1 [[Hit Point|Hit Points]].
**Failure:** The target gains 1 [[Hit Point]] and it's [Dying](Dying) condition is replaced with the [Feeble](Feeble) condition.
**Critical Failure:** No effect.
