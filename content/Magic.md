Divination - Magic used to gain knowledge or insight about the past, present, or future. This can include techniques like scrying, tarot reading, and augury.

Elemental - Magic used to manipulate the elements such as earth, air, fire and water. This can include creating storms, and producing flame.

Evocation - Magic used to create destructive force of pure arcane. This can include spells that conjure pure beams or explosion of arcane.

Necromancy - Magic used to inflict deadly effects and control of undead creatures. This can include life siphoning and raising undead creatures.

Alteration - Magic used to change or manipulate existing things. This can include altering time, gravitation, objects or person's properties.

Enchantment - Magic used to imbue objects or people with magical properties. This can include creating magical weapons, and talismans.

Creation - Magic used to create things out of thin air. This can include creating food, water, and other supplies out of nothing.

Summoning - Magic used to call forth creatures or entities from other worlds of existence. This can include summoning demons, angels, or spiritual beings.

Illusion - Magic used to create sensory experiences that are not real. This can include creating realistic illusions of people, places, or things.

Charms - Magic used to affect the behavior or emotions of others. This can include spells that make someone fall in love, become more courageous, or forget something.

Curses - Magic used to harm or punish others. This can include spells that cause physical or emotional pain, or that bring bad luck.

Restoration - Magic used to heal or restore things. This can include healing spells for injuries or diseases.

Warding - Magic used to protect oneself or others from harm. This can include creating arcane barriers and protection against other arcane.
