_[[1st Level Spell]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: F, V, S
**Mana**:: 1
**Range**:: 20 metres
**Duration**:: 10 minutes

**Effect**:: You create an illusory image that fits in a 3-metre cube within range.

As a [[Double Action]] you cause the image to move within range. As it changes location, you can alter its appearance so that its movements appear natural.

Physical interaction with the image reveals the illusion. A [[Creature]] that takes the [[Inspect]] action to examine the image can determine that it is an illusion with a successful [[Investigation]] [[Check]] against your [[Spell Save DC]]. If a [[Creature]] discerns the illusion for what it is, the creature can see through the image.

**Upcasting**:: For each additional mana spent, the cube's dimensions increases by 1 metre. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 3 extra mana.

**Type**:: [[Projection Magic]]
