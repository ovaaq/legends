Although **Legends RPG** is a classless game, many players enjoy familiar fantasy archetypes. Using [[Talent|Talents]], you can recreate these classic concepts or combine them in unique ways. Below are a few common examples.

### Warrior

These are the martial masters that now how to use weapons and armour to the maximum. They know martial techniques that are combat tricks. Their strikes are powerful and they easily critically wound enemies in melee or ranged.
