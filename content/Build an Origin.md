**Saving Throws.** Distribute 4 [[Rank|Ranks]] among [[Saving Throw|Saving Throws]].

**General Skill Ranks.** Distribute 10 [[Rank|Ranks]] among [[General Skill|General Skills]].

**Expert Skill Ranks.** Distribute 4 [[Rank|Ranks]] among [[Expert Skill|Expert Skills]].

**Language.** Addition to [[Common]], you can learn one language from the cultural area you have been raised or you had motivation to learn.

**Occupation.** Describe what your character did before beginning their adventure. This can be a profession, vocation, lifestyle, or other significant role.

> examples:
> _Acolyte, Apothecary, Artisan, Bard, Beast Tamer, Blacksmith, Bounty Hunter, Charlatan, Cook, Courier, Farmer, Feylost, Fisher, Gladiator, Gravekeeper, Hermit, Hunter, Merchant, Miner, Monk, Noble, Pirate, Prisoner, Sailor, Scholar, Scribe, Shepherd, Shipwright, Smuggler, Soldier, Thief, Urchin, Woodsman_

**Wealth.** you can choose to become

- **Low:** 10 gp
- **Average:** 75 gp
- **High:** 250 gp
  more you can get from [[Increased Skill]] talent.
