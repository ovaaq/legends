**Requirements**:: [[Lucky]]
**Cost**:: 3 LP

*Flavour text*.

You gain 1 additional Luck Point.

**Share Luck.** When a creature you can see makes an [[Attack Roll]], [[Skill]] [[Check]], or [[Saving Throw]], you can use your [[Reaction]] and spend 1 Luck Point to roll an additional d20 and use the highest result. You can choose to spend the Luck Point after rolling the die, but before the outcome is determined.
