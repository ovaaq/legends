Each spell has between one and four components. A spellcaster must fulfil all of the spell’s listed components to cast the spell.

- [[Focus Component]] (F): The spellcaster must hold a [[Spellcasting Focus]] in one hand.
- [[Verbal Component]] (V): The spellcaster must be able to speak the spell’s magical words.
- [[Somatic Component]] (S): The spellcaster must have at least one hand free to perform the spell’s gestures.
- [[Material Component]] (M): The spellcaster must possess the materials listed in the spell. The spell specifies whether these materials are consumed when cast.
