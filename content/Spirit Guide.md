**Requirements**:: [[Spiritual Connection]]
**Cost**:: 2 LP

_Flavour text_.

You gain 2 additional Spirit Points.

**Guidance.** When you make [[Skill]] [[Check]] using [[Intelligence]], you may spend 1 Spirit Point to add 1d6 to the roll. You can choose to do so after rolling but before the outcome is determined.

**Protection.** When you take damage, you may use your [[Reaction]] to spend 1 Spirit Point to reduce the damage by 2d6 + your [[Sorcery]] score. If your Sorcery score is negative, treat it as 0 for this feature.
