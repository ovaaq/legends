_[[Cantrip]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: F, V, S
**Mana**:: 0
**Range**:: 20 metres
**Duration**:: Instant

**Effect**:: You release a wave of force toward a [[Creature]] within range. Make a [[Ranged Spell Attack]] against the target. On a hit, the target takes 1d8 [[Bludgeoning Damage]] and is pushed 2 meters away from you if its [[Creature Size]] is [[Large]] or smaller. On a [[Critical Hit]], the target is pushed 4 meters, or 2 meters if its [[Creature Size]] is [[Huge]].

**Upcasting**:: For each 1 additional mana spent, increase the damage by 1d6.

**Type**:: [[Distortion Magic]]
