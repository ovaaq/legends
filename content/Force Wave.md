_[[Cantrip]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: F, V, S
**Mana**:: 0

**Range**:: 20 metres
**Duration**:: Instantaneous

**Effect**:: You release a wave of force toward a creature within range. Make a [[Ranged Spell Attack]] against the target. On a hit, the target takes 1d8 [[Bludgeoning Damage]] and is pushed 4 meters away from you if its [[Creature Size]] is [[Large]] or smaller. On a [[Critical Hit]], the target is pushed 8 meters, or 4 meters if its [[Creature Size]] is [[Huge]].

**Upcasting**:: For each additional mana spent, the damage increases by 1d6. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 3 extra mana.
