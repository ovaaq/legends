**Requirements**:: [[Mobile]]
**Cost**:: 1 LP

_Lore_

You can move through the space of any [[Creature]] whose [[Creature Size]] is the same size or smaller than yours. Moving through a creature’s space in this way costs no additional movement. You cannot end your movement in another creature’s space.
