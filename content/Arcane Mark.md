_[[Cantrip]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: F, V, S
**Mana**:: 0

**Range**:: Touch
**Duration**:: 7 days

**Effect**:: You imbue a nonmagical object with magical markings. The object gains one of the following properties:

- **Light**: Emits bright light in a 1-metre radius and dim light for an additional 1 metre.
- **Recorded Message**: When tapped, the object plays a recorded message you speak as you cast (up to 6 seconds long), audible within 2 metres.
- **Persistent Sensory Effect**: The object continuously emits an odour or nonverbal sound of your choice (wind, waves, chirping, etc.) perceivable up to 2 metres.
- **Visual Marking**: A static visual effect appears on one surface of the object. This can be a simple picture, up to 25 words of text, lines, shapes, or a combination.

**Upcasting**:: For each additional mana spent, the duration increases by 7 days. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 3 extra mana.
