- **Combat Stabilization**  
    Stabilizing a dying ally is a quick action instead of a full one.

- **Adrenal Shot**  
    Revive a fallen ally with temporary health and increased movement, but they suffer exhaustion afterward.

- **Pain Suppression**  
    Remove stun, fear, or pain-based penalties when healing an ally.

- **Mass Triage**  
    Heal or stabilize multiple allies at reduced effectiveness.

- **Auto-Response Training**  
    When an ally nearby drops, you may immediately move toward them without provoking reactions.
