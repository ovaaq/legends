_[[Cantrip]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: V, S
**Mana**:: 0
**Range**:: 4 metres
**Duration**:: Instant

**Effect**:: As you hold your hand, a blinding light shoots forth from your palm. Each creature in a 4-metre cone must make a [[Constitution Saving Throw]]:

**Success:** Target takes no damage.
**Failure:** Target takes 1d4 [[Fire Damage]].
**Critical Failure:** Target takes 1d4 [[Fire Damage]] and is [[Blinded]] until the end of your next turn.

**Upcasting**:: For each additional two mana spent, the damage increases by 1d6. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 4 extra mana.

**Type**:: [[Evocation Magic]]
