_Flavour text_.

To not mess with balance you can use only non metallic materials in your weapons or [[Unarmed Strike]]s.

Unarmed Strikes deal 1d4 [[Strength]] damage.

**Ki Points.** Your [[Ki Point Maximum]] equals your [[Sorcery]]. You expend [[Ki Point|Ki Points]] to perform [[Ki Technique|Ki Techniques]] and regain all expended [[Ki Point|Ki Points]] when you complete a [[Long Rest]].

**Ki Techniques.** [[Ki Technique|Ki Techniques]] are specialised combat manoeuvres learned through training and experience. Each technique belongs to one of four categories: [[Offensive Technique|Offensive]], [[Defensive Technique|Defensive]], [[Control Technique|Control]], or [[Support Technique|Support]].

Select two [[Ki Technique|Ki Techniques]] from the following list and add them to your [[Learned Ki Techniques]].

- ki ki

**Perform Technique.** You can perform any of your [[Learned Ki Techniques]] by paying its [[Ki Point]] cost.
