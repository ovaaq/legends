_Flavour text_.

**Living Suit.** As an [[Action]], activate your symbiote for 10 minutes. It covers your body and carried items. While transformed, your [[Armour Class]] and [[Evasion Class]] each become 16 if lower, and you gain [[Protective]] (2). You cannot benefit from worn [[Armour]] while transformed, but you can still benefit from a [[Shield]]. You can use this ability twice per [[Long Rest]]. You can dissmiss living armour with [[Action]].
