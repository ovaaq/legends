_Carved from the heart of stone itself, dwarves are a long lasting and enduring folk. Their bond with the earth runs deep; stone, metal, and gem feel as much a part of them as flesh and blood. Though their origins lie in the deep places of the world, dwarves have spread far across the realms, adapting to mountain strongholds, bustling cities, and distant frontiers alike._
