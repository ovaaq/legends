_[[Adventuring Item]], [[Equipment]]_

_Have you ever counted how many balls once pouch contains?_

**Weight**:: 1 Stack
**Cost**:: 1 gp

As an [[Action]], you can spill Caltrops from their pouch. They spread to cover a level, 1-square-meter area within 2 meters of yourself.

**Hazard:** The affected area is considered [[Difficult Terrain]].

**Spiky Floor:** Any [[Creature]] that enters the area for the first time on a [[Turn]] or begins their [[Turn]] in the area must succeed on a DC 15 [[Agility Saving Throw]] or take 1 [[Piercing Damage]] and its movement immediately ends. Furthermore, until the [[Creature]] regains at least 1 [[Hit Point]], its [[Walking Speed]] is reduced by 2 meters.

**Cautious Movement.** A [[Creature]] moving through the area at half speed doesn't need to make the [[Saving Throw]].

**Recovery:** Recovering the scattered Caltrops takes 5 minutes.
