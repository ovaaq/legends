_[[Cantrip]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: V, S
**Mana**:: 0

**Range**:: Melee
**Duration**:: Instant

**Effect**:: You channel magic, collecting necrotic energy on your palm. Make a [[Touch Spell Attack]] against a target within reach. On a hit, the target takes 1d12 [[Necrotic Damage]]. If you score a [[Critical Hit]], the target is [[Frightened]] until the start of your next turn.

**Upcasting**:: For each additional mana spent, the damage increases by 1d6. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 3 extra mana.
