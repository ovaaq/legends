_[[Metamagic]]_

When you cast a [[Spell]] that forces other [[Creature|Creatures]] to make a [[Saving Throw]], you can protect some of those [[Creature|Creatures]] from the spell’s full force. To do so, spend 1 [[Metamagic Point]] and choose a number of those [[Creature|Creatures]] up to your [[Sorcery]] (minimum of one [[Creature]]). A chosen [[Creature]] automatically [[Critical Success|Critically Succeeds]] on its [[Saving Throw]] against the [[Spell]].
