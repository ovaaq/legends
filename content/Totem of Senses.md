| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Eagle's Sight**       | You gain [[Darkvision]] within **10 metres**. You can also discern distant creatures and objects as though they were half as far away.                                                                                                                     |
| **Wolf's Scent**        | You can identify familiar creatures by scent and track creatures whose scent trail is no more than **24 hours old**. Within **10 metres**, you can detect the presence and direction of creatures whose scent can reach you, but not their exact location. |
| **Spider's Vibrations** | You gain [[Tremorsense]] within **10 metres** while touching the ground or a connected solid surface. This sense cannot detect flying or incorporeal creatures.                                                                                            |
| **Bat's Echolocation**  | You gain [[Blindsight]] within **10 metres** while you can hear and freely produce sound. This sense does not function while you are [[Muted]], [[Deafened]], or within magical silence.                                                                   |
| **Elephant's Hearing**  | You can hear sounds from **twice their normal distance**. Within **10 metres**, you can determine the exact location of creatures making audible sounds, even if you cannot see them.                                                                      |
