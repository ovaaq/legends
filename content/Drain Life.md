_[[3rd Level Spell]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: V, S
**Mana**:: 3
**Range**:: touch
**Duration**:: Instant

**Effect::** You reach toward a [[Creature]] and drain its life force. Make a [[Touch Spell Attack]] against the target. On a hit, the target takes 6d6 [[Necrotic Damage]], and you restore [[Hit Point|Hit Points]] equal to half the damage dealt.

**Upcasting**:: For each additional mana spent, the damage increases by 1d6. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 3 extra mana.

**Type**:: [[Removal Magic]]
