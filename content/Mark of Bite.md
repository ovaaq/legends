**Cost**:: 1 LP

_The mark of the bite awakens your body. Thirst rises, and blood calls to you._

**Altered Physiology.** You no longer require air or food to sustain yourself.

**Hunger of Blood.** You must consume fresh blood from a living, warm-blooded creature at least once every 7 days.

If you go longer than 7 days without feeding, your predatory instincts begin to take hold. For each day beyond the seventh without feeding, make a [[Charisma Saving Throw]] (DC 12 + days since your last feed) at random hour to resist your instincts:

**Critical Success:** You maintain full control and do not make a saving throw the next day.
**Success:** You maintain full control.
**Failure:** You must start to hunt a living creature that you can see within 100 meters.
**Critical Failure:** You immediately attack the nearest living creature.
