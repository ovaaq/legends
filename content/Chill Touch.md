_[[Cantrip]]_

**Casting**:: [[Double Action]]
**Components**:: V, S
**Mana**:: 0
**Range**:: Touch
**Duration**:: Instant

**Effect**:: You channel magic, causing frost to gather in your palm, freezing cold. Make a [[Touch Spell Attack]] against a target within reach. On a hit, the target takes 1d10 [[Cold Damage]].

If you score a [[Critical Hit]], the target’s [[Walking Speed]] is reduced by 4 metres until the start of your next [[Turn]].

**Upcasting**:: For each 1 additional mana spent, increase the damage by 1d6.

**Type**:: [[Evocation Magic]]
