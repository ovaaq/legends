**Cost**:: 1 LP

_You pledge yourself to a mighty dragon, receiving a fragment of its power in exchange for tribute and service._

Increase your [[Invocation]] [[Rank]] by 1.

**Draconic Patron.** Choose one of the following dragons as your patron. Your chosen patron determines your associated [[Damage Type]] and which patron-specific spells are added to your [[Pact Spell List]].

| Patron       | Damage Type          |
| ------------ | -------------------- |
| Frost Dragon | [[Cold Damage]]      |
| Sea Dragon   | [[Acid Damage]]      |
| Storm Dragon | [[Lightning Damage]] |
| Fire Dragon  | [[Fire Damage]]      |

**Draconic Mark.** Your patron leaves a visible mark upon you, such as patches of scales, draconic eyes, horns, claws, or another feature appropriate to the dragon.

**Draconic Resilience.** When you take damage of your patron’s associated [[Damage Type]], you can use a [[Reaction]] to gain [[Resistance]] against the triggering damage. You can use this ability a number of times equal to your [[Invocation]] [[Rank]] per [[Long Rest]].

**Tribute.** Your patron expects regular tribute, such as gold, gemstones, valuable objects, rare knowledge, trophies, defeated enemies, or acts of service. Determine the nature of this tribute with your [[Game Master]] when you gain this talent.

When any of your pact talents requires a [[Creature]] to make a [[Saving Throw]], it uses your [[Pact Save DC]].
