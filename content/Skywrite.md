_[[1st Level Spell]]_

**Casting**:: 10 minutes
**Components**:: F, S
**Mana**:: 1

**Range**:: Sight
**Duration**:: 24 hours

**Effect**:: You create visible clouds in a portion of the sky within your sight, forming either a symbol or a message of up to ten words. The formation remains fixed in place for the duration. A strong wind may scatter the formation prematurely, ending the spell early.

**Upcasting**:: For each additional mana spent, the duration increases by 24 hours. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 3 extra mana.
