_[[Cantrip]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: F, V, S
**Mana**:: 0
**Range**:: Touch
**Duration**:: 1 hour

**Effect**:: You touch one [[Object]] that is no larger than 2 metres in any dimension. Until the spell ends, the [[Object]] sheds [[Bright Light]] in a 4-metre radius and [[Dim Light]] for an additional 4 metres. The light can be coloured as you like. Completely covering the object with something opaque blocks the light. The spell ends if you cast it again or dismiss it as an [[Action]].

If you target an [[Object]] held or worn by a hostile [[Creature]], that [[Creature]] must succeed on a [[Agility Saving Throw]] to avoid the spell.

**Upcasting**:: For each additional mana spent, the duration increases by 2 hours. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 3 extra mana.

**Type**:: [[Projection Magic]]
