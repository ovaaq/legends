**Requirements**:: [[Elevated Strength]]
**Cost**:: 1 LP

_Cold and eternal, your body knows no decay. With a taste of your blood, even the strongest mind can fall under your sway._

**Finalised Transformation**

- You become creature type [[Undead]], [[Vampire]].
- You no longer age naturally.
- You are immune to disease and poison.
- Your heartbeat, breath, and body warmth vanish.
- You regain 1 HP each hour.

**Blood Charm**. You bite a [[Humanoid]] and inject part of your blood. The creature must make a [[Charisma Saving Throw]] DC 16 or become charmed by you. A charmed creature remains under your influence for 1d4 days.

**Group**:: Innate
**Following Abilities**:: [[Bat Form]]
