### Property

- **Protective** – resistance, armor, barriers
- **Restorative** – healing, recovery, cleansing
- **Enhancing** – strengthens or improves something
- **Transformative** – changes form, size, substance
- **Controlling** – restraining, slowing, manipulating
- **Deceptive** – illusions, concealment, disguises
- **Mobilizing** – speed, flight, teleportation
- **Summoning** – creating or calling creatures/objects
- **Disruptive** – dispelling, weakening, suppressing
- **Revealing** – detection, perception, divination
