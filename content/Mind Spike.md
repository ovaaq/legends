_[[2nd Level Spell]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: F, V, S
**Mana**:: 2
**Range**:: 20 metres
**Duration**:: Instant

**Effect**:: You drive a mental spike into the mind of a [[Creature]] you can see within range. Target must make a [[Intelligence Saving Throw]].

**Critical Success:** No effect.
**Success:** A target takes half damage.
**Failure:** A target takes 3d8 [[Psychic Damage]].
**Critical Failure:** A target takes double the damage and gains [[Dazed]] until the start of your [[Turn]].

**Upcasting**:: For each 1 additional mana spent, increase the damage by 1d6.

**Type**:: [[Distortion Magic]]
