**Requirements**:: [[Experimental Elixir]]
**Cost**:: 2 LP

_Flavour text_.

Whenever you create an experimental elixir, you can roll twice and choose which effect it has.

| d8  | Effect                                                                                          |
| :-: | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  1  | **Endless Air.** The drinker can hold their breath indefinitely for 1 hour.                     |
|  2  | **Swiftness.** The drinker's [[Walking Speed]] increases by 2 metres for 1 hour.                |
|  3  | **Resilience.** The drinker gains a +1 bonus to [[Saving Throw\|Saving Throws]] for 10 minutes. |
|  4  | **Flight.** The drinker gains a [[Flying Speed]] of 2 metres for 10 minutes.                    |
|  5  | **Vitality.** The drinker gains 2d6 + 2 [[Temporary Hit Point\|Temporary Hit Points]].          |
|  6  | **Vigour.** The drinker can add 1d4 to every [[Attack Roll]] for 1 minute.                      |
|  7  | **Reflex.** The drinker's [[Evasion Class]] is increased by 2 for 1 minute.                     |
|  8  | **Scales.** The drinker's [[Armour Class]] is increased by 2 for 1 minute.                      |
