_[[3rd Level Spell]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: V, S, M\*
**Mana**:: 3
**Range**:: 2 metres
**Duration**:: Instant

_\*[[Corpse]]._

**Effect**:: You grant a corpse within range a semblance of life and intelligence, allowing it to answer up to five questions. The [[Corpse]] must have a mouth, can't be [[Undead]], and can't have been targeted by this [[Spell]] within the last 10 days.

The corpse knows only what it knew in life and speaks only languages it knew. Its answers are typically brief, cryptic, or repetitive. It isn't compelled to answer truthfully if it recognizes you as an enemy or considers you hostile.

This [[Spell]] doesn't return the [[Corpse|Corpse's]] [[Soul]]. It can't learn new information, know what has happened since its death, or speculate about future events.

**Upcasting**:: For each additional mana spent, the amount of questions increases by 1. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 3 extra mana.

**Type**:: [[Revelation Magic]]
