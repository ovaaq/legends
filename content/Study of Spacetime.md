_Lore._

**Portal Jumper.** You can spend 3 metres of [[Walking Speed]] to teleport to an unoccupied space you can see within 3 metres of yourself. You can use this benefit a number of times equal to your [[Sorcery]] but only once per [[Turn]], and you regain all expended uses when you finish a [[Long Rest]].
