**Requirements**:: [[Arcane Spellcasting]]
**Cost**:: 2 LP

_Warding is the branch of arcane study dedicated to protection and the mitigation of harm. Practitioners seek to understand how objects and creatures can better withstand damage and how to neutralize destructive spells before they manifest._

**Protective Ward.** As a [[Triple Action]], you conjure a protective barrier around a [[Creature]] you can see. While the barrier is active, the [[Creature|Creature's]] [[Armour Class]] increases by 1, or by 2 if they are not wearing any [[Armour]]. The barrier lasts for 8 hours or until you dismiss it as a [[Free Action]]. Only one [[Creature]] can be affected by this barrier at a time. If you conjure it again, the previous barrier dissipates.
