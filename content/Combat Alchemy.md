**Requirements**:: [[Alchemy Initiate]]
**Cost**:: 2 LP

_You have learned how to improvise damage from reagents themselves._

**Combat Alchemy.** Select one [Reagent](Reagent) you possess and one [Creature](Creature) within 4 metres. The target makes a [Constitution Saving Throw](Constitution%20Saving%20Throw). If you choose a curative reagent, no [Saving Throw](Saving%20Throw) is required.

**Critical Success:** No effect.
**Success:** A target takes half damage.
**Failure:** A target takes takes indicated amount.
**Critical Failure:** A target takes double damage.
