_[[Offensive Action]]_

**Execution**:: [[Double Action]]
**Target**:: [[Creature]]

You attempt to disarm an [[Object]] held by a [[Creature]]. Make an [[Athletics]] or [[Sleight of Hand]] [[Check]]. The target makes an [[Agility Saving Throw]] or [[Strength Saving Throw]] against the result of your [[Check]].

**Success:** No effect.
**Failure:** The [[Object]] drops in an unoccupied space within 1 metre of the target. Alternatively, if you have a free hand, you can grab the [[Object]] instead.
