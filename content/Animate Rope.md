*[[1st Level Spell]]*

**Casting**:: [[Double Action]]
**Components**:: F, V, S, M*
**Mana**:: 1

**Range**:: 10 metres
**Duration**:: Concentration, up to 10 minutes

*\*Rope*

**Effect**:: You animate a rope, causing it to behave as a snake and use the stat block of an [[Animated Rope]]. On your turn, you can use an [[Action]] to mentally command the rope, causing it to act as though it were taking its own turn.

The spell lasts until the rope is destroyed or the spell ends, at which point the rope becomes nonmagical and inanimate once more.

**Upcasting**:: For each additional mana spent, the you can animate one additional rope. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 3 extra mana.
