### Character Creation

**[[Attribute]].** The 8 core attributes that determine your character's natural capabilities in different areas.

**[[Ancestry]].** Your character's ancestry, such as [[Human]] or [[Dwarf]]. You can also combine ancestries to create characters such as a half-dwarf.

**[[Level]].** Ranges from 1 to 30 and indicates how legendary your character has become.

**[[Level Up]].** When your character gains a [[Level]], they gain 5 [[Legend Point|Legend Points]]. Nothing else automatically improves.

**[[Legend Point]].** Meta currency gained when you [[Level Up]]. You spend [[Legend Point|Legend Points]] on [[Talent|Talents]].

**[[Talent]].** A core improvement that develops or grants your character's capabilities, such as [[Increased Health]] or [[Arcane Spellcasting]].

**[[Talent Group]].** A structured group of related [[Talent|Talents]].

**[[Talent Tree]].** An organized progression of [[Talent|Talents]] that visually displays their prerequisites and connections.

**[[Hit Point]].** Represents how much damage you can withstand. When you reach 0 [[Hit Point|Hit Points]], you begin dying.

**[[Resource]].** A pool spent to fuel your abilities. Resources include [[Mana]], [[Stamina Point|Stamina Points]], [[Blood Dice]], [[Haste Point|Haste Points]], [[Faith Point|Faith Points]], and others.

**[[Speed]].** Determines how far you can move. Your primary Speed is typically your [[Walking Speed]].

**[[Evasion Class]].** Represents how difficult you are to hit. Your Evasion Class equals 10 + [[Agility]], but can be improved by [[Increased Evasion]] and other effects.

**[[Armour Class]].** Represents how difficult your physical protection is to overcome. An unarmoured humanoid has an [[Armour Class]] of 8.

### Playing

**[[Check]].** Roll 1d20 and add modifiers to determine the success or failure of a [[Skill Check]], [[Attack Roll]], or [[Saving Throw]].

**[[Difficulty Class]].** The target number a [[Check]] must meet or exceed to succeed.

**[[General Skill]].** A broad skill that any character can use, such as [[Acrobatics]], [[Athletics]], or [[History]].

**[[Expertise]].** Specialized training that adds a bonus to a relevant [[Skill Check]].

**[[Skill Check]].** A [[Check]] that uses a [[General Skill]] to determine whether you succeed.

**[[Saving Throw]].** A [[Check]] made to resist or avoid an effect imposed upon you.

**[[Advantage]].** Roll 2d20 for a [[Check]] and use the higher result.

**[[Disadvantage]].** Roll 2d20 for a [[Check]] and use the lower result.

### Combat

**[[Encounter]].** A scene where time must be tracked precisely, typically during combat, a chase, or another dangerous situation. At the beginning of an [[Encounter]], each participant makes an [[Initiative]] [[Check]] to determine the [[Initiative Order]]. During each [[Round]], every participant takes one [[Turn]].

**[[Initiative Order]].** The order in which participants take their [[Turn|Turns]] during an [[Encounter]].

**[[Round]].** A 6 second cycle during which every participant takes one [[Turn]]. After the final [[Turn]], a new [[Round]] begins from the top of the [[Initiative Order]].

**[[Turn]].** Your time to act during a [[Round]]. On your [[Turn]], you can move and use 3 [[Action|Actions]], and [[Free Action|Free Actions]]. You can also use one [[Reaction]] per [[Round]] when its trigger occurs.

**[[Action]].** A measure of the time and effort required to perform something. Activities can cost 1 to 3 Actions, such as making a [[Weapon Attack]], [[Spell Attack]], or [[Sprint]].

**[[Reaction]].** A response to a specific trigger that can be anytime during [[Round]].

**[[Free Action]].** A minor activity that requires little time or effort and costs no [[Action|Actions]], such as dropping an object.

**[[Flat-Footed]].** You are disoriented until the start of your first [[Turn]] in an [[Encounter]]. While [[Flat-Footed]], you cannot use [[Reaction|Reactions]].
