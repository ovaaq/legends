**Requirements**:: [[Shield the Innocent]]
**Cost**:: 3 LP

_Flavour text_.

You gain 2 additional [[Faith Point|Faith Points]].

**Rebuke Aggression.** When an aggressor you can see within 6 metres causes another [[Creature]] to lose [[Hit Point|Hit Points]], you can use your [[Reaction]] and spend 1 [[Faith Point]] per 10 [[Hit Point|Hit Points]] lost, rounded up. Those [[Hit Point|Hit Points]] become the rebuke damage, and the aggressor makes a [[Charisma Saving Throw]] against your [[Divine Spell Save DC]].

**Critical Success:** The attacker is unaffected.
**Success:** The attacker takes half the rebuke damage as [[Radiant Damage]].
**Failure:** The attacker takes the rebuke damage as [[Radiant Damage]].
**Critical Failure:** The attacker takes twice the rebuke damage as [[Radiant Damage]].
