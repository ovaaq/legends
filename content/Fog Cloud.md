_[[1st Level Spell]]_

**Casting**:: [[Double Action]]
**Components**:: F, V, S
**Mana**:: 1
**Range**:: 40 metres
**Duration**:: [[Concentration]], 1 hour

**Effect**:: You create a 4-metre-radius sphere of fog centred on a point within range. The sphere spreads around corners, and its area is heavily obscured. It lasts for the duration or until a wind of moderate or greater speed (at least 5 metres per second) disperses it.

**Upcasting**:: For each additional mana spent, the radius of the fog increases by 2 metres. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 3 extra mana.

**Type**:: [[Conjuration Magic]]
