_[[Equipment]]_

_Great products, pricy and slow to make, but definitely useful stuff._

Alchemical items are divided into three categories: [[Potion|Potions]], [[Poison|Poisons]], and [[Alchemical Item|Alchemical Items]]. Potions grant beneficial effects to those who drink them, while Poisons are detrimental or even lethal. Alchemical Items cover everything else, including exploding vials, area effects, and concoctions that affect terrain or multiple targets.
