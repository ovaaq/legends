_Flavour text_.

**Expanded Runes.** Your maximum number of active [[Shield Rune|Shield Runes]] increases to 8.

**Transfer Runes.** You can use an [Action](Action.md) to transfer up to 3 active [[Shield Rune|Shield Runes]] to another [Creature](Creature.md). The runes last for 1 hour or until the [Creature](Creature.md) takes damage from an attack, hazard, or spell. The next time the [Creature](Creature.md) takes damage from one of these sources, roll 1d6 and subtract the result from the damage. The [[Shield Rune|Shield Runes]] then vanish.
