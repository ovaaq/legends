_Flavour text_.

Animals react differently to you. [[Natural Beast|Natural Beasts]] that would normally be [[Hostile]] treat you as [[Neutral]], and those that would normally be neutral treat you as [[Friendly]]. In addition, you gain 1d4 on [[Animal Handling]] Checks when interacting with [[Natural Beast|Natural Beasts]].
