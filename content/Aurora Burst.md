*[[1st Level Spell]]*

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: F, V, S, M*
**Mana**:: 0

**Range**:: 20 metres
**Duration**:: Instantaneous

*\*100 grams of [[Gold Dust]], consumed by the spell*

**Effect**:: You launch a dazzling array of flashing, colorful light. Each [[Creature]] in a 6-meter [[Cone]] originating from you must succeed on a [[Constitution Saving Throw]] or have the [[Blinded]] condition until the end of your next turn.

**Upcasting**:: For each additional 2 mana spent, the duration increases by one turn. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 4 extra mana.
