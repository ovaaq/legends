### 1. **Intimidation First**

Most conflicts never become lethal.

- Raised hackles, stiff tail, bared teeth
- Growling, snapping the air
- Dominance is often settled by posture alone\
  👉 Wolves prefer submission over injury.

### 2. **Pack Coordination**

Wolves almost never fight alone.

- They **circle and pressure** enemies or prey
- Individuals take roles: flankers, chasers, harassers
- They rotate to avoid exhaustion\
  👉 The pack is the weapon.

### 3. **Targeted Biting**

When violence happens, it’s precise:

- **Neck and throat** (to cut off air or blood flow)
- **Hamstrings and hind legs** (to cripple prey)
- **Muzzle and face** (against other wolves)\
  They don’t trade blows—they **disable**.

### 4. **Hit-and-Run Tactics**

Especially against larger foes:

- Quick lunges → retreat → repeat

- Force the enemy to turn, stumble, or panic

- Let exhaustion do the work\
  👉 Wolves win by stamina, not strength.
