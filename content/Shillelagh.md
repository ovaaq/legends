_[[Cantrip]]_

**Casting**:: [[Action]]
**Components**:: V, S, M\*
**Mana**:: 0
**Range**:: Touch
**Duration**:: 1 minute

_\*Wooden [[Club]] or [[Quarterstaff]]_

**Effect**:: The wood of a club or quarterstaff you are holding is imbued with magical power. For the duration, you can use your [[Spell Attack Modifier]] for the attack and [[Sorcery]] as damage rolls of melee attacks using that weapon, and the weapon’s damage die becomes a d8. The weapon also becomes magical, if it isn’t already. The spell ends if you cast it again or if you let go of the weapon.

**Upcasting**:: For each two additional mana spent, weapon's damage increases by 1d6. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 4 extra mana.

**Type**:: [[Enchantment Magic]]
