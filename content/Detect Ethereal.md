_[[1st Level Spell]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: F, V, S, M\*
**Mana**:: 0

**Range**:: 20 metres
**Duration**:: Instantaneous

**Effect**:: For the duration, you sense the location of any [[Celestial]], [[Elemental]], [[Fey]] or [[Fiend]] within 12 meters of yourself. You also sense whether the [[Hallow]] or [[Desecrate]] spell is active there and, if so, where.

The spell is blocked by 30 centimetres of stone, dirt, or wood; 2.5 centimetres of metal; or a thin sheet of lead.

**Upcasting**:: For each additional mana spent, the range increases by 4 meters. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 3 extra mana.
