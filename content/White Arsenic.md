_[[Adventuring Item]], [[Equipment]]_

_Lore._

**Weight**:: 1 Stack
**Cost**:: 2 gp

If a Creature eats or drinks this poison, it must make a _DC 15 Constitution Saving Throw_.

_Critical Success:_ No effect.

_Success:_ The target is _Poisoned_ for 1 hour.

_Failure:_ The target is _Poisoned_ for 2d12 hours.

_Critical Failure:_ The target is _Poisoned_ for double the failure duration.

While _Poisoned_ by this substance, the Creature takes 1d4 Poison Damage each hour, beginning immediately.
