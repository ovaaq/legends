_[[Utility Action]]_

**Execution**:: [[Double Action]]
**Target**:: [[Dying]] [[Creature]]

You attempt to stabilize a [[Dying]] [[Creature]]. Make a [[Medicine]] [[Check]] DC 10.

**Critical Success:** The target gains 1 [[Hit Point]] and loses the [[Dying]] condition.
**Success:** The target gains 1 [[Hit Point]] and it's [Dying](Dying) condition is replaced with the [Feeble](Feeble) condition.
**Failure:** No effect.
**Critical Failure:** Target’s [[Dying]] level increases by 1.

If you expend one use of a [[Healer's Kit]], treat your result as one tier higher.
