**Action Cost**:: [[Double Action]]
**Target**:: Dying Creature

You attempt to stabilize a [[Dying]] [[Creature]]. Make a [[Medicine]] [[Check]] DC 10:

**Critical Success:** Target regains 1 [[Hit Point|HP]].
**Success:** Target's [[Dying]] condition  ends and it remains [[Unconscious]] for 1d4 hours.
**Failure:** No effect.
**Critical Failure:** Target’s [[Dying]] condition increases by 1.

If you expend one use of a [[Healer's Kit]], treat your result as one tier higher.
