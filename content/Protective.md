[[Armour]] reduces incoming [[Slashing Damage]], [[Piercing Damage]] and [[Bludgeoning Damage]] by the level of Protective (x). Does not work against [[Falling Damage]].

When you take damage, first subtract your [[Protective]] value from the damage. Then apply [[Resistance]] and any other damage modifiers.
