_[[Adventuring Item]], [[Equipment]]_

_Lore._

**Weight**:: 1 Stack
**Cost**:: 2 gp

You can use the [[Poison]] in this vial to coat one [[Weapon]] that deals [[Piercing Damage]] or [[Slashing Damage]] or up to three pieces of [[Ammunition]]. Applying the [[Poison]] takes a [[Double Action]]. [[Creature]] hit by the poisoned [[Weapon]] or [[Ammunition]] takes 1d4 [[Poison Damage]]. Once applied, the [[Poison]] retains potency for 1 minute before drying.

_Coat Weapon._ As a _Double Action_, you can apply Venom Oil to coat one weapon that deals Piercing or Slashing damage, or up to five pieces of Ammunition.

A creature hit by the coated weapon or ammunition takes _1d4_ Poison damage. Once applied, the poison retains its potency for 1 minute before drying.
