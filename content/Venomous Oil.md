**Type**:: [[Wound Poison]]
**Weight**:: 0.1 kg

You can use the [[Coat Weapon]] action to apply Venomous Oil to one weapon that deals [[Piercing Damage|Piercing]] or [[Slashing Damage]], or up to five pieces of [[Ammunition]].

A [[Creature]] hit by the coated [[Weapon]] or [[Ammunition]] takes 1d4 [[Poison Damage]]. Once applied, the [[Poison]] retains its potency for 1 minute before drying.
