_[[Wound Poison]]_

_Is it green?_

**Cost**:: 1 gp
**Weight**:: 0.1 kg
**Duration**:: 1 minute

You can use the [[Coat Weapon]] action to apply [[Venomous Oil]] to one [[Weapon]] that deals [[Piercing Damage|Piercing]] or [[Slashing Damage]], or up to five pieces of [[Ammunition]]. Once applied, the [[Poison]] retains its potency for 1 minute before drying.

A [[Creature]] hit by the coated [[Weapon]] or [[Ammunition]] takes 1d4 [[Poison Damage]].
