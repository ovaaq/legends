_[[1st Level Spell]]_

**Casting**:: [[Reaction]]\*
**Components**:: F, S
**Mana**:: 2

**Range**:: 4 metres
**Duration**:: Instantaneous

_\*When a [[Creature]] you can see within range takes [[Elemental Damage]]._

**Effect**:: The spell devours some of the incoming elemental energy, lessening its effect. Target has [[Resistance]] to the triggering [[Damage Type]] until the start of your next turn.

**Upcasting**:: For each additional 2 mana spent, you can target one additional creature. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 4 extra mana.
