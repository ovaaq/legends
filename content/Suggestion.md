_[[2nd Level Spell]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: F, V, S
**Mana**:: 2
**Range**:: 10 metres
**Duration**:: 8 hours

**Effect**:: Choose a [[Creature]] within range that understand you, and suggest a reasonable, non-harmful course of activity. The target must make a [[Charisma Saving Throw]]. It makes it with [[Advantage]] if you or your allies are fighting it.

**Success:** No effect.\
**Failure:** The target is [[Charmed]] and realizes it was magically manipulated when the [[Spell]] ends.\
**Critical Failure:** The target is [[Charmed]] and does not realize it was magically manipulated.

The [[Spell]] ends when the suggestion is completed or if you or your allies damage the target.

**Upcasting**:: For each 2 additional mana spent, you can target one additional [[Creature]].

**Type**:: [[Enchantment Magic]]
