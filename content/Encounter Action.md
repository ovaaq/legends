### Encounter Turn Structure

On your [[Turn]], you have access to the following resources:

- **3 Actions:** Spent to perform activities. An activity can require a [[Action]], [[Double Action]], or [[Triple Action]].
- **Movement:** Spent to travel up to your [[Walking Speed]].
- **1 Reaction:** Used once per [[Round]] in response to a specific trigger, even outside your turn. You regain your [[Reaction]] at the start of your next [[Turn]].
- **Free Actions:** Minor activities that cost no actions or resources.

### Encounter Action Types

Actions are categorized by their function during combat:

- [[Offensive Actions]]: Attacking, controlling, or harming enemies (e.g., striking, tripping).
- [[Defensive Actions]]: Avoiding, mitigating, or protecting against incoming harm.
- [[Magical Actions]]: Casting spells, channelling supernatural abilities, or activating magic items.
- [[Movement Actions]]: Tactical positioning and physical manoeuvres (e.g., jumping, dashing, hiding).
- [[Utility Actions]]: Miscellaneous tasks (e.g., administering first aid, drinking a potion).
