*[[1st Level Spell]]*

**Casting**:: [[Double Action]]
**Components**:: F, V, S
**Mana**:: 1

**Range**:: Touch
**Duration**:: Instantaneous

**Effect**:: A [[Creature]] you touch regains a number of [[Hit Point|Hit Points]] equal to 2d8 plus your [[Sorcery]].

**Upcasting**:: For each additional mana spent, the healing increases by 1d8. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 3 extra mana.
