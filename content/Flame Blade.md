_[[Cantrip]]_

**Casting**:: [[Action]]
**Components**:: V, S, M\*
**Mana**:: 0

**Range**:: Touch
**Duration**:: 1 minute

_\*[[Melee Weapon]]_

**Effect**:: You enchant a weapon you are holding, causing it to erupt in flames for up to 1 minute or until you do a [[Melee Attack]] with it. On a hit, the attack deals an additional 1d6 [[Fire Damage]].

**Upcasting**:: For each additional mana spent, the damage increases by 1d6. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 3 extra mana.
