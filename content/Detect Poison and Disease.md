**CAST TIME**: ▶▶▶
**MANA**: 0
**RANGE**: 20 meters
**SPELL PARTS**: F, V, S, M\*
**LEVEL**: [[1st Level Spell]]
**DURATION**: Instantaneous

**EFFECT**: For the duration, you sense the location of poisons, poisonous or venomous creatures, and magical contagions within 12 meters of yourself. You sense the kind of poison, creature, or contagion in each case.

The spell is blocked by 30 centimetres of stone, dirt, or wood; 2.5 centimetres of metal; or a thin sheet of lead.

**UPCASTING**: For each additional mana spent, the range increases by 4 meters. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 3 extra mana.
