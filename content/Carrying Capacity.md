| Weight         | Approx. Mass | Description                                                                  |
| -------------- | ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Negligible** | < 1 kg       | Tiny, light, or incidental items; carried freely and ignored for encumbrance |
| **1 Stack**    | ~2.5 kg      | Light gear; easily carried or worn                                           |
| **2 Stacks**   | ~5 kg        | Bulky or dense items; noticeable weight                                      |
| **3 Stacks**   | ~7.5 kg      | Heavy gear; awkward without proper packing                                   |
| **4 Stacks**   | ~10 kg       | Very heavy; requires a pack, harness, or both                                |
| **5 Stacks**   | ~12.5 kg     | Extreme load; strains the body                                               |
| **6 Stacks**   | ~15 kg       | Massive load; impractical without aid                                        |
| **7 Stacks**   | ~17.5 kg     | Very large pack; carrying anything more slows movement noticeably            |
| **8 Stacks**   | ~20 kg       | Maximum comfortable load for average adult                                   |
| **9 Stacks**   | ~22.5 kg     | Overloaded; moving becomes difficult, strain builds                          |
| **10 Stacks**  | ~25 kg       | Plate armor; extremely heavy gear                                            |

Carrying Capacity = 10 + 2 × [[Strength]] score
