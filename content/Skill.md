Skills define your character’s capabilities. They are divided into three types:

- [[General Skill|General Skills]]: Common activities available to any adventurer.
- [[Expert Skill|Expert Skills]]: Specialized skills that require more training.
- [[Special Skill|Special Skills]]: Unique abilities, such as spellcasting or martial skill.

Each skill has a [[Rank]] that reflects your character’s level of proficiency.
