A [[Dazed]] [[Creature]] cannot use [[Reaction|Reactions]] and has [[Disadvantage]] on [[Attack Roll|Attack Rolls]], [[Agility]] [[Check|Checks]], and [[Agility Saving Throw|Agility Saving Throws]]. When it attempts to cast a [[Spell]] other than a [[Cantrip]], it must roll a d20. On a 10 or lower, the [[Spell]] fails and its [[Mana]] is spent.
