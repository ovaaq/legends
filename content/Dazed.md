- A dazed [[Creature]] cannot take [[Reaction|Reactions]] and has [[Disadvantage]] on [[Agility]] [[Check|Checks]] and [[Agility Saving Throw|Agility Saving Throws]].
- [[Melee Attack|Melee Attacks]] against the [[Creature]] have [[Advantage]], and the creature’s own [[Attack Roll|Attack Rolls]] have [[Disadvantage]].
- When a dazed [[Creature]] attempts to cast a [[Spell]] other than a [[Cantrip]] it must roll a d20. On an 10 or lower the [[Spell]] doesn’t take effect and the [[Mana]] is wasted.
- When a dazed [[Creature]] takes the [[Dash]] action they must succeed on DC 15 [[Agility Saving Throw]] or fall [[Prone]].
