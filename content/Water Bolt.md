_[[Cantrip]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: V, S
**Mana**:: 0
**Range**:: 20 metres
**Duration**:: Instant

**Effect**:: You hurl a tiny bolt water at a creature within range. Make a [[Ranged Spell Attack]] against the target. On a hit, the target takes 1d8 [[Bludgeoning Damage]].

If you score a [[Critical Hit]] the target is [[Muted]] until the start of your next turn.

**Upcasting**:: For each 1 additional mana spent, increase the damage by 1d6.

**Type**:: [[Conjuration Magic]]
