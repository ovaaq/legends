**Spellbook.** You gain [[Spellbook]], which is used to record your spells. Select four [[Cantrip|Cantrips]] and two [[1st Level Spell|1st Level Spells]] from your [[Arcane Spell List]] and add them to your [[Spellbook]].

**Preparing Spells.** You can prepare [[Spell|Spells]] after completing a [[Long Rest]] or spending 2 hours studying your [[Spellbook]]. When preparing spells, choose a number of [[Spell|Spells]] from your [[Spellbook]] equal to 2 + [[Intelligence]] + [[Spellcraft]] [[Rank]] and add them to your [[Prepared Arcane Spells]]. You can prepare two [[Cantrip|Cantrips]] in place of one prepared [[Spell]].

**Mana.** You use [[Mana]] to power your [[Spell]]. Your [[Mana Point Maximum]] equals your [[Sorcery]] score. You regain all expended [[Mana]] after a [[Long Rest]].

**Casting Spells.** You can cast any [[Spell]] on your [[Prepared Arcane Spells]]. The maximum amount of [[Mana]] you can spend on a [[Spell]] equals your [[Spellcraft]] [[Rank]].

**Ritual Casting.** You can cast any [[Spell]] from your [[Spellbook]] as a ritual. Doing so increases the casting time by 10 minutes.

**Copying a Spell.** When you find a [[Spellbook]] or [[Spell Scroll]], you can add it to your spellbook if it is of a spell level you can prepare and if you can spare the time to decipher and copy it.

Copying a spell into your spellbook involves reproducing the basic form of the spell, then deciphering the unique system of notation used by the wizard who wrote it. You must practice the spell until you understand the sounds or gestures required, then transcribe it into your spellbook using your own notation.

For each level of the spell, the process takes 2 hours and uses 1 pot of [[Magical Ink]]. Also make [[Spellcraft]] [[Check]] DC 10 + level of the spell. on success you learn it and can add it to your spellbook. on failure you loose your magical ink and time was wasted. This represents studying and practising the spell. Once you have spent this time and ink, you can add it to your [[Spellbook]].
