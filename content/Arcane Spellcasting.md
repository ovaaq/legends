**Spellbook.** You gain [[Spellbook]], which is used to record your [[Spell|Spells]]. Select four [[Cantrip|Cantrips]] and two [[1st Level Spell|1st Level Spells]] from your [[Arcane Spell List]] and add them to your [[Spellbook]].

**Preparing Spells.** You can prepare [[Spell|Spells]] after completing a [[Long Rest]] or spending 2 hours studying your [[Spellbook]]. choose a number of [[Spell|Spells]] from your [[Spellbook]] equal to 2 + [[Intelligence]] + [[Spellcraft]] [[Rank]] and add them to your [[Prepared Arcane Spells]]. You can prepare two [[Cantrip|Cantrips]] in place of one prepared [[Spell]].

**Casting Spells.** You can cast any [[Spell]] on your [[Prepared Arcane Spells]]. The maximum amount of [[Mana]] you can spend on a [[Spell]] equals your [[Spellcraft]] [[Rank]].

**Ritual Casting.** You can cast any [[Spell]] from your [[Spellbook]] as a ritual. Doing so increases the casting time by 10 minutes.
