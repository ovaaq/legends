An [[Immobilized]] [[Creature]] has a [[Walking Speed]] of 0 and cannot perform [[Action|Actions]] or [[Reaction|Reactions]] that require physical movement. Its [[Evasion Class]] becomes 5, [[Attack|Attacks]] against it have [[Advantage]], and it automatically critically fails [[Agility Saving Throw|Agility Saving Throws]]. Any [[Spell]] with a [[Somatic Component]] automatically fails.
