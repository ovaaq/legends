An Attack is an attempt to harm a target with a [[Weapon]], [[Spell]], or another offensive effect.
Attack Procedure

1. Choose an Attack.
2. Choose an eligible target.
3. Pay the Attack's Action cost.
4. Make an [[Attack Roll]].
5. Compare the result with the target's indicated Defensive Class.
6. Determine whether the Attack misses, hits, or scores a [[Critical Hit]].
7. On a hit, make a [[Damage Roll]] and resolve any additional effects.

#### Making Attack Roll

[[Attack Roll]]

**Weapon Attack Roll** = d20 + [[Strength]] / [[Precision]] + [[Martial Skill]] [[Rank]]
**Spell Attack Roll** = d20 + [[Sorcery]] + [[Spellcasting Skill]] [[Rank]]

For example:

- Hitting with Mace = d20 + 3([[Strength]]) + 2([[Martial Skill]] [[Rank]])
- Stabbing with a Dagger = d20 + 2([[Precision]]) + 4([[Martial Skill]] [[Rank]])
- Casting Fire Bolt = d20 + 3([[Sorcery]]) + 2([[Spellcasting Skill]] [[Rank]])

#### Critical Hit

You score a [[Critical Hit]]:

- When you [[Attack Roll]] is 10 higher than the [[Armour Class]] / [[Evasion Class]].
- You roll [[Natural 20]] and hit the target.

#### Damage Roll

[[Damage Roll]]

- [[Damage Type]]

**Weapon Damage** = Weapon's Damage Die + [[Strength]] / [[Precision]] / Weapon's Damage Type
**Spell Damage** = Spell's Damage Die / Spell's Damage Type

For example:

- Damage with Mace = 1d8 + 3([[Strength]]) [[Bludgeoning Damage]]
- Stabbing with a Dagger = 1d4 + 2([[Precision]]) [[Piercing Damage]]
- Casting Fire Bolt = 3d4 [[Fire Damage]]

#### Damage Roll with Critical Hit

Normal [[Damage Roll]] + maximum from the Damage Dice.

For example:

- Critical Hit with Mace = 1d8 + 3([[Strength]]) + 8(1d8 Maxed) [[Bludgeoning Damage]]
- Critical Hit with a Dagger = 1d4 + 2([[Precision]]) + 4(1d4 Maxed) [[Piercing Damage]]
- Critical Hit Fire Bolt = 3d4 + 12(3d4 Maxed) [[Fire Damage]]
