**Requirements**:: [[Alchemy Initiate]]
**Cost**:: 2 LP

_There is always some leftovers and with curiosity you can always combine reagents to produce experiments._

Whenever you finish a [[Long Rest]], you can produce an experimental elixir in an empty flask. Roll on the Experimental Elixir table for the elixir's effect, which is triggered when someone drinks the elixir.

You can create additional _experimental elixirs_ by expending two [[Common Reagent|Common Reagents]]. When you do so, you use your action to create the elixir in an empty flask you touch, and you choose the elixir's effect from the Experimental Elixir table.

Creating an _experimental elixir_ requires you to have [[Alchemist’s Supplies]] on your person, and any elixir you create lasts until it is drunk or until the end of your next [[Long Rest]].

| d6  | Effect                                                                                                     |
| :-: | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  1  | **Swiftness.** The drinker's [[Walking Speed]] increases by 2 metres for 1 hour.                           |
|  2  | **Flight.** The drinker gains a [[Flying Speed]] of 2 metres for 10 minutes.                               |
|  3  | **Resilience.** The drinker gains a +1 bonus to [[Saving Throw\|Saving Throws]] for 10 minutes.            |
|  4  | **Vigour.** The drinker can roll a d4 and add the number rolled to every [[Attack Roll]] and for 1 minute. |
