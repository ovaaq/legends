_Flavour text_.

You gain 2 additional [[Spirit Point|Spirit Points]].

**Turn Spirits.** As a [[Double Action]]. Spend 1 [[Spirit Point]] to force all [[Spirit|Spirits]] within 10 meters that can see or hear you to make a [[Charisma Saving Throw]]. On a failure, a [[Spirit]] flees for 1 minute or until it takes damage.

While fleeing, the [[Spirit]] must use its movement to travel as far from you as possible and cannot willingly move within 10 meters of you. It cannot take [[Reaction|Reactions]], and its action can only be used to [[Dash]] or escape a movement-restricting effect. If unable to move, it must use the [[Dodge]] action.
