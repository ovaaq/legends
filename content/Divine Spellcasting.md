**Requirements**:: [[Divine Acolyte]]
**Cost**:: 5 LP

_Flavour text_.

Increase your [[Prayer]] [[Rank]] by 1.

These spells are added to your [[Divine Spell List]].

##### Cantrips

- [[Close Wounds]]
- [[Glow]]
- [[Guiding Strike]]
- [[Sacred Flame]]
- [[Thaumaturgy]]
- [[Thunderbolt]]

##### 1st Level Spells

- [[Bless]]
- [[Minor Conjuration]]
- [[Guiding Bolt]]
- [[Healing Word]]
- [[Detect Undead]]

**Preparing Spells.** You can prepare spells after completing a [[Long Rest]]. When preparing spells, choose a number of spells from your [[Divine Spell List]] equal to 1 + [[Prayer]] [[Rank]] + [[Intelligence]] and add them to your [[Prepared Divine Spells]] list. You can prepare two [[Cantrip|Cantrips]] in place of one prepared [[Spell]].

> [!info] Prepared Spells
> Number of Prepared Divine Spells = 1 + [[Prayer]] [[Rank]] + [[Intelligence]]

**Casting Spells.** You can cast any [[Spell]] on your [[Prepared Divine Spells]]. The maximum amount of [[Mana]] you can spend on a [[Spell]] equals your [[Prayer]] [[Rank]].

**Holy Symbol.** You can use a [[Holy Symbol]] as a [[Spellcasting Focus]] for [[Divine Spellcasting]].
