_[[3rd Level Spell]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: F, V, S
**Mana**:: 3
**Range**:: Touch
**Duration**:: 1 hour

**Effect**:: You touch a [[Willing]] [[Creature]], placing it in a cataleptic state indistinguishable from death. The target appears dead to physical inspection and magic used to determine its status.

The target is [[Blinded]] and [[Incapacitated]], its [[Walking Speed]] becomes 0, and it has [[Resistance]] to all damage except [[Psychic Damage]]. Diseases and poisons affecting the target are suspended until the [[Spell]] ends. The target does not age or require sustenance while in this state.

As an [[Action]], you can touch the target to end the [[Spell]].

**Upcasting**:: For each 1 additional mana spent, increase the duration by 1 hour.

**Type**:: [[Warding Magic]]
