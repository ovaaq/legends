_[[6th Level Spell]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: F, V, S
**Mana**:: 6
**Range**:: 10 metres
**Duration**:: Instant

**Effect**:: You point at a [[Creature]] or [[Object]] within range, firing a red beam from your finger. Make a [[Ranged Spell Attack]] against the target.

On a hit, the target takes 10d6 + 20 [[Necrotic Damage]]. If this damage reduces a [[Creature]] to 0 [[Hit Point|Hit Points]], the [[Creature]] and everything [[Non-magical]] it is wearing or carrying are disintegrated into fine grey dust.

If the target is a [[Non-magical]] [[Object]] or a creation of magical force, it is disintegrated on a hit. If it is too large to be entirely disintegrated, only a 3-metre [[Cube]] of it is destroyed. [[Magical]] [[Object]] are unaffected.

**Upcasting**:: For each 1 additional mana spent, increase the damage by 1d6.

**Type**:: [[Decay Magic]]
