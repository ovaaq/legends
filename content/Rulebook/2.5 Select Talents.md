Choosing [[Talent|Talents]] is one of the most important steps in character creation. First, spend a minimum of 5 [[Legend Point|LP]] on [[Ancestry Talent|Ancestral Talents]]. You then have 15 [[Legend Point|LP]] remaining, or 16 if you did not choose [[Ancestry|Additional Ancestry]], to spend on any [[Talent|Talents]] for which your character meets the requirements.

If you prefer, you may save some of your unspent [[Legend Point|LP]] and record them on your [[Character Sheet]]. You can spend these points later to unlock [[Talent|Talents]].

Here is an example [[Talent]] to walk through. This is the [[Lucky]] [[Talent]]:

> [!quote] Lucky
> ![[Lucky]]

**Requirements.** Any prerequisites that must be met before this [[Talent]] can be acquired. These may include other [[Talent|Talents]], a minimum [[Level]], a specific [[Ancestry]], or other statistics. [[Lucky]] has no prerequisites, so anyone can acquire it.

**Cost.** The number of [[Legend Point|LP]] required to acquire this [[Talent]]. [[Talent|talents]] cost 1 to 4 LP. [[Lucky]] is a powerful [[Talent]], so it has a cost of 4 [[Legend Point|LP]].

**Flavour Text.** A short piece of thematic text that captures the essence of the [[Talent]].

**Description.** Describes all mechanical benefits, effects, and rules provided by the [[Talent]].

**Tree.** Indicates the [[Talent Tree]] to which this [[Talent]] belongs. [[Lucky]] belongs to the [[Luck Talent Tree]].

**Group.** Indicates the [[Talent Group]] to which this [[Talent]] belongs. [[Lucky]] belongs to the [[General Talent]] group.

**Following.** Lists the [[Talent|Talents]] that become available after acquiring this [[Talent]]. [[Lucky]] unlocks [[Shared Luck]].
