Choosing [[Talent|Talents]] is one of the most important steps in character creation. After selecting your [[Ancestry Talent|Ancestral Talents]], you have 15 [[Legend Point|LP]] remaining. You can spend these points to acquire any [[Talent|Talents]] for which your character meets the requirements. If you prefer, you may save some unspent [[Legend Point|LP]] and record it on your [[Character Sheet]] for later use.

**Requirements.** Any needed requirements your character must have before taking this talent. These typically are other [[Talent|Talents]], [[Level]], [[Ancestry]] or other statistics.

**Cost.** indicates how many [[Legend Point|Legends Points]] must be spent to acquire the [[Talent]].

**Flavour Text.** Provides little thematic text related to the talent.

**Description.** provides all mechanical benefits and acquired rules

**Tree.** Shows which [[Talent Tree]] this [[Talent]] belongs.

**Group.** Shows which [[Talent Group]] this [[Talent]] belongs.

**Following.** provides a what talent open new for you which have this one as requirement.
