Choosing [[Talent|Talents]] is one of the most important steps in character creation. After selecting your [[Ancestry Talent|Ancestral Talents]], you have 15 [[Legend Point|LP]] remaining. You can spend these points to acquire any [[Talent|Talents]] for which your character meets the requirements. If you prefer, you may save some unspent [[Legend Point|LP]] and record it on your [[Character Sheet]] for later use.

**Requirements.** Any prerequisites that must be met before this [[Talent]] can be acquired. These may include other [[Talent|Talents]], a minimum [[Level]], a specific [[Ancestry]], or other statistics.

**Cost.** The number of [[Legend Point|Legend Points]] required to acquire this [[Talent]]. Talents cost 1 to 4 LP.

**Flavour Text.** A short piece of thematic text that captures the essence of the [[Talent]].

**Description.** Describes all mechanical benefits, effects, and rules gained.

**Tree.** Indicates the [[Talent Tree]] to which this [[Talent]] belongs.

**Group.** Indicates the [[Talent Group]] to which this [[Talent]] belongs.

**Following.** Lists [[Talent|Talents]] that become available after acquiring this [[Talent]].
