Your character's [[Ancestry]] defines their physical traits and lineage. It influences their appearance, lifespan, [[Creature Size]], [[Speed]], and grants access to [[Ancestry Talent|Ancestry Talents]].

### Choose Primary Ancestry

Every character must choose a primary [[Ancestry]]. This [[Ancestry]] represents the character's dominant heritage and it determines your character's [[Creature Size]], [[Walking Speed]] and lifespan. Options are following:

### Additional Ancestry

You may choose an additional [[Ancestry]] to represent a mixed heritage. This [[Ancestry]] may be selected from the list below. Some [[Ancestry|Ancestries]] can alter your [[Walking Speed]] or [[Creature Size]]. Characters with two [[Ancestry|Ancestries]] have physical features and lifespans that fall somewhere between those of their parent ancestries.

An additional [[Ancestry]] allows you to represent lineages such as a Human with Celestial blood or a Dwarf-Giant hybrid. It also makes combinations such as a half-Dwarf, half-Elf character possible.

If you choose not to select an additional Ancestry, you gain 1 additional [[Legend Point]] to spend when choosing [[Talent|Talents]].
