Choosing [[Talent|Talents]] is one of the most important steps in character creation. After selecting your [[Ancestry Talent|Ancestral Talents]], you have 15 [[Legend Point|LP]] remaining. You can spend these points to acquire any [[Talent|Talents]] for which your character meets the requirements. If you prefer, you may save some unspent [[Legend Point|LP]] and record it on your [[Character Sheet]] for later use.

**Requirements** lists any prerequisites needed to acquire the Talent. These may include other Talents, a minimum Level, a specific Attribute score, a Skill Rank, or other conditions.

**Cost** indicates how many **Legend Points (LP)** must be spent to acquire the [[Talent]].

**Description** provides a brief overview of the [[Talent]], including any flavour text and its mechanical effects.

**Tree** provides info which tree this talent belongs.

**Group** provides info which group it belongs.

**Following** provides a what talent open new for you which have this one as requirement.

### Talent Groups

### Ancestry Talents

[[Ancestry Talent|Ancestral Talents]] require your character to have the matching ancestry. They provide thematic abilities and features that reflect your character’s lineage.

### General Talents

[[General Talent|General Talents]] are common and straightforward. They allow you to acquire additional Hit Points, learn new skills, or improve your Saving Throws.

### Exploration Talents

[[Adventuring Talent]] represent abilities developed during adventuring and exploration, helping characters navigate, survive, and interact with the world.

### Social Talents

[[Social Talent]] focus on persuasion, deception, diplomacy, and other skills for navigating social interactions.

### Martial Talents

[[Martial Talent]] reflect a character’s combat prowess, from mastering weapons to harnessing inner energy and unleashing devastating attacks. These Talents help warriors excel on the battlefield and adapt to any fight.

- [[Weapon Talent Tree]]
- [[Armour Talent Tree]]
- [[Martial Arts Talent Tree]]
- [[Berserk Talent Tree]]

### Arcane Talents

[[Arcane Talent]] represent a character’s mastery of magic, combining study and arcane discipline. There is a General Talent Tree for abilities useful to any arcane scholar. Each special study also has its own Talent Tree.

- [[Arcane Talent Tree]]
- [[Spellblade Talent Tree]]
- [[Warding Talent Tree]]

### Divine Talents

[[Divine Talent]] are powers granted by higher powers. They provide strength and abilities for followers and champions to protect and defend others. There is a General Talent Tree for abilities useful to any follower, and each deity has its own Talent Tree for unique Talents tied to their domain.

- [[Divine Talent Tree]]
- [[Knowledge Talent Tree]]
- [[Light Talent Tree]]

### Innate Talents

[[Innate Talent]] reflect powers that are inherent or arise spontaneously, tied to a character’s nature, heritage, or chaotic magical force.

- [[Vampire Talent Tree]]
- [[Wild Magic Talent Tree]]

### Pact Talents

[[Pact Talent]] are powers gained through bonds with otherworldly entities or magical pacts. They grant unique abilities and enhancements to those who strike deals with forces beyond them.

- [[Draconic Pact Talent Tree]]
- [[Hexcore Talent Tree]]

### Resonant Talents

[[Resonant Talent]] represent abilities that harmonize with the world through music, movement, or other expressive forms. These Talents allow characters to influence, inspire, and shape their surroundings.

- [[Art of Melody Talent Tree]]
- [[Art of Motion Talent Tree]]

### Spiritual Talents

[[Spiritual Talent]] reflect a character’s connection to the unseen spiritual world. They allow characters to channel spiritual energy, commune with spirits, and wield spiritual forces. There is a General Talent Tree for any practitioner, plus specialty Talent Trees for unique abilities.

- [[Spiritual Talent Tree]]
- [[Nature Talent Tree]]
- [[Elemental Talent Tree]]

### Spellcasting Talents

[[Spellcasting Talent]] are general Talents that enhance a character’s spellcasting abilities, making their magic more potent, versatile, and effective.
