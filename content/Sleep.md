_[[1st Level Spell]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: F, V, S
**Mana**:: 1
**Range**:: 10 metres
**Duration**:: [[Concentration]], 1 hour

**Effect**:: Choose one [[Creature]] you can see within range. The target makes a [[Constitution Saving Throw]]. The target has [[Advantage]] on this saving throw if it is fighting you or your allies.

**Critical Success:** No effect.
**Success:** The target is [[Drowsy]] for 1 hour.
**Failure:** The target falls [[Asleep]] for the duration.

A [[Creature]] affected by this spell wakes if it takes damage, or if another [[Creature]] uses an [[Action]] to shake or slap it awake.

**Upcasting**:: For each additional mana spent, you can target one additional [[Creature]]. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 3 extra mana.

**Type**:: [[Enchantment Magic]]
