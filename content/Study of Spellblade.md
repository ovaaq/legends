**Requirements**:: [[Arcane Spellcasting]]
**Cost**:: 2 LP

_Your inspiration from martial weaponry has lead to study of spellblade, branch of arcane study where  mana become extension of your arms._

**Spellblades.** As an [[Action]], you conjure up to two weapons of pure magical energy. They last for 1 hour or until you dismiss them as a [[Free Action]]. When you conjure a spellblade, choose its form and [[Damage Type]]. Its damage type can be [[Piercing Damage]], [[Bludgeoning Damage]], or [[Slashing Damage]].

Spellblades cannot be disarmed and appear as solid weapons made of glowing arcane force. They count as a [[Simple Focus]] for you, and you can perform [[Somatic Spell Part]] while holding them.

When you make a [[Weapon Attack]] with a spellblade, you can use your [[Melee Spell Attack]] modifier instead of your [[Weapon Attack]] modifier. Attacks made with spellblades are still [[Weapon Attack|Weapon Attacks]].
