_[[1st Level Martial technique]]_

**Execution**:: [[Free Action]]
**Stamina**:: 1
**Range::** Melee
**Weapon**:: -
**Duration::** Instant

**Effect**:: When you hit a [[Creature]] with a [[Melee Weapon Attack]] or [[Unarmed Strike]], you can expend 1 Stamina Point to strike at a balance point to force it to make a [[Agility Saving Throw]]:

**Critical Success:** No effect.\
**Success:** Target makes [[Attack Roll|Attack Rolls]] with disadvantage until the end of its next turn.
**Failure:** Target falls [[Prone]].
**Critical Failure:** Target falls [[Prone]], and takes 1d6 [[Bludgeoning Damage]].
