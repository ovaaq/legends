**Requirements**:: [[Magic Awareness]]
**Cost**:: 1 LP

_Magical disturbances spread wider._

**Unstable Aura.** Whenever any other [[Creature]] within 1 metre of you casts a [[1st Level Spell]] or higher, roll a d20. On a roll of 1, wild magic affects it's spell. Roll a d100 and consult the [[Wild Magic Surge Table]] to determine the effect. If the effect is a spell that normally requires [[Concentration]], it instead lasts for its full duration without requiring [[Concentration]].
