_[[1st Level Alchemical Formula]]_

**Preparation Time**:: 1 hour
**Equipment**:: [[Alchemist’s Supplies]]
**Range**:: Touch
**Duration**:: Instant

**Ingredients**:: 1 [[Common Inert Reagent]], 1 [[Common Volatile Reagent]], [[Glass Vial]]

**Effect**:: You attempt to craft a [[Strength Potion]]. Make an [[Crafting]] ([[Alchemy]]) [[Check]] DC 15.

**Critical Success:** You create 2 [[Strength Potion|Strength Potions]] instead of 1.
**Success:** You create 1 [[Strength Potion]].
**Failure:** Time is wasted, but ingredients are saved.
**Critical Failure:** Ingredients are lost. Roll 1d12 on the [[Alchemy Hazard Table]].
