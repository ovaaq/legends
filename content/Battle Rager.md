_Flavour text_.

**Rage Points.** You have 2 [[Rage Point|Rage Points]], which fuel your rage abilities. You regain all expended [[Rage Point|Rage Points]] when you finish a [[Long Rest]].

**Rage.** As an [[Action]], you can expend 1 [[Rage Point]] to enter a rage. While raging:

- You have advantage on [[Strength]] [[Check|Checks]] and [[Strength]] Saving Throws.
- Your [[Strength]] is temporarily increased by 1.
- You can't cast [[Spell|Spells]] or maintain [[Concentration]] on them.

Your Rage lasts for 10 minutes. It ends early if you become [[Unconscious]] or if you end your [[Turn]] without doing at least one of the following since the end of your previous Turn:

- Make an [[Attack]] against a hostile creature.
- Force a hostile creature to make a Saving Throw.
- Take damage.
- Use a Bonus [[Action]] to maintain your Rage.

You can also end your Rage on your Turn as an [[Action]].
