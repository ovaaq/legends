**Requirements**:: [[Path of Afterlife]]
**Cost**:: 3 LP

_Flavour text_.

You gain 2 additional [[Spirit Point|Spirit Points]].

**Ghost Form.** As and [[Action]], you can spend 1 [[Spirit Point]] to assume a semi-ethereal, ghostlike form, along with any [[Object|Objects]] you are carrying. This form lasts for 10 minutes, until you dismiss it as a [[Action]] or become [[Unconscious]] or [[Dead]].

While [[Ghost Form]] is active:

- Your [[Walking Speed]] is halved.
- You gain [[Resistance]] to [[Physical Damage]].
- You are [[Immune]] to being [[Grappled]], [[Restrained]], or otherwise physically bound.
- You have disadvantage to any [[Strength]], [[Agility]], [[Precision]] based [[Attack|Attacks]], [[Check|Checks]] and [[Saving Throw|Saving Throws]].
