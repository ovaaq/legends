Your character's background is a collection of characteristics that represent the place and occupation that were most formative before your character embarked on a life of adventure. Each background includes a brief narrative of what your character’s past might have been like.

### Parts of a Background

A background includes the following:

**Ability Scores.** A background lists four [[Ability Score|Ability Scores]]. Increase three of them by 1.

**Saving Throws.** A background lists three [[Saving Throw|Saving Throws]]. Increase two of them by 1 [[Rank]].

**General Skill Ranks.** Distribute 4 [[Rank|Ranks]] among a list of [[General Skill|General Skills]] listed in background.

**Expert Skill Ranks.** Distribute 3 [[Rank|Ranks]] among a list of [[Expert Skill|Expert Skills]] listed in background.

**Talent.** A background grants a free [[Talent|Talent]] or two.

**Equipment.** A background provides free [[Equipment]].

**Initial Wealth.** A background specifies character’s starting wealth.
