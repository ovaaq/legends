*[[1st Level Spell]]*

**Casting**:: 1 minute
**Components**:: V, S
**Mana**:: 1

**Range**:: Touch
**Duration**:: 8 hours

**Effect**:: You touch one willing creature. The target gains two guidance charges. Before the spell ends, it can expend one charge to roll a d4 and add the result to a [[Check]] of its choice. The die may be rolled before or after making the [[Check]]. The spell ends once all charges are expended.

**Upcasting**:: For each additional mana spent, the amount of guidance charges increases by 1. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 3 extra mana.
