_[[1st Level Spell]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: V, S
**Mana**:: 1
**Range**:: Touch
**Duration**:: 8 hours

**Effect**:: You touch one [[Willing]] [[Creature]]. The target gains two motes of guidance. Before the spell ends, the target can expend 1 mote before making a [[Check]] to add 1d4 to the result.

**Upcasting**:: For each additional mana spent, the amount of motes increases by 1. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 3 extra mana.

**Type**:: [[Revelation Magic]]
