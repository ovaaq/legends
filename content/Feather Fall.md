_[[1st Level Spell]]_

**Casting**:: [[Reaction]]\*
**Components**:: F, V
**Mana**:: 1

**Range**:: 20 metres
**Duration**:: 10 minutes

_\*When a [[Creature]] within range of you falls._

**Effect**:: Choose up to five falling [[Creature|Creatures]] within range. A falling [[Creature|Creature’s]] rate of descent slows to 2 metres per [[Round]] until the spell ends. If the [[Creature]] lands before the spell ends, it takes no [[Falling Damage]] and can land on its feet, and the spell ends for that [[Creature]].

**Upcasting**:: For each additional mana spent, the amount of affected [[Creature|Creatures]] increase by 5. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 3 extra mana.
