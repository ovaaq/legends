_[[1st Level Spell]]_

**Casting**:: [[Reaction]]\*
**Components**:: F, V
**Mana**:: 1

**Range**:: 20 metres
**Duration**:: 10 minutes

_\*When you or a creature within range of you falls_

**Effect**:: Choose up to five falling creatures within range. A falling creature’s rate of descent slows to 2 metres per round until the spell ends. If the creature lands before the spell ends, it takes no falling damage and can land on its feet, and the spell ends for that creature.

**Upcasting**:: For each additional mana spent, the amount of affected creatures increase by 5. You can upcast the spell with a maximum of 3 extra mana.
