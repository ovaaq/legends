_[[3rd Level Spell]]_

**Casting**:: [[Triple Action]]
**Components**:: F, V, S, M\*
**Mana**:: 3
**Range**:: 10 metres
**Duration**:: Permanent

_\*2 [[Karat|Karats]] of [[Ruby]], consumed by the [[Spell]]._

**Effect**:: Choose a [[Humanoid]] that you can see within range. The target must make a [[Charisma Saving Throw]].

On [[Failure]] the target becomes [[Cursed]]. Choose one [[Minor Curse]].

- **Clumsiness.** Target has −1 penalty to [[Attack Roll|Attack Rolls]].
- **Lethargy.** Target gains 1 level of [[Exhausted]] at [[Dawn]].
- **Marked.** You always know the target's location.
- **Nightmares.** Target's cannot have [[Good Rest]].
- **Weakness.** Target has −1 penalty to [[Saving Throw|Saving Throws]].

**Type**:: [[Decay Magic]]
